Terms for drivers

DRAFT for the lawyer (driver-terms-draft-1): not in force. In the test environments no money moves.

Who we are

Stavia is a platform: it shows you spaces that places offer and takes the payment for them. The parking itself is the place's service, under its rules shown before you book; Stavia's fee is for the booking.

The booking and its price

You see the whole price for the time you chose, with Stavia's fee, before you pay. Once paid, the booking is confirmed; the confirmation and the payment's document are in the app and in your e-mail.

Cancelling

You cancel for free until the time each place states; after it the booking is not refunded. If the place cannot take you in, you get all your money back.

The stay

Leave by the end of your booking, or add time in the app while the place allows it. Time beyond the booking is paid at the place's rate.

Your car

A place looks after cars only where it says it is guarded. Stavia is not liable for damage to the car or things in it, except where the law makes it liable.

Complaints

Report a problem from the booking: it is registered with a number and you get a document of it. We answer within 30 days, usually much sooner.

Your data

What we keep, why and for how long is in Privacy. A place sees only your first name and your car's plate.
