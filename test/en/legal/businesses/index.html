Terms for businesses

DRAFT for the lawyer (business-terms-draft-1): not in force. In the test environments no money moves.

The parties

Stavia is a platform that brings drivers to the business's places and takes their payments on its behalf. The business provides the parking and is responsible for the space, the entrance and its staff.

The listing

The business sets its prices, hours and rules; a change applies only to new bookings. Stavia checks a new place before drivers see it, and the business keeps what it states true.

Money

Stavia keeps a commission set in the tariff the business accepted (a starting rate first, with its end date), and pays out the rest monthly with a statement; the commission invoice comes by the 5th of the next month.

How places are shown

Drivers see the places that fit their time and car, ordered by price or walking time as they choose. No place pays to be shown higher in the pilot; ratings do not change the order.

Suspending a listing

Stavia suspends a listing only with a reason it states, decided by a person, and the business can contest it (Regulation (EU) 2019/1150).

Data

The business receives a driver's first name and car plate to let them in, as an independent controller for that; Stavia keeps the rest as Privacy says.

Changes and ending

Stavia announces a change to these terms at least 15 days ahead, and the business may end the contract before it applies. Either party may end it with 30 days' notice.
