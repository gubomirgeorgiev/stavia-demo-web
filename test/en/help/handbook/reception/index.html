Today

What it is

Your places’ day on one screen: what needs you now, who is inside, who is coming today and who has left. The days ahead are under Bookings.

Where

Stavia Business opens here: Today is first in the bottom bar (on a laptop, in the menu on the left). The owner, the manager, reception and the staff at the entrance (the “Entrance” role) see it. The accountant has no Today: their Stavia Business opens on Money. With several places the screen gathers them all and names the place on each booking.

On the screen

Under the title are the date and time and short counts: how many need you, how many of the spaces offered to Stavia are free now, how many cars are inside and how many are coming today.

  1. Today: … — how many bookings start today. The owner and the manager also see what the places earn from them; the other roles see only the number.
  2. Let a car in by its plate — opens Entry, where you let a car in by its booking’s code or its plate.
  3. +1 car without Stavia — notes a car let in outside Stavia, for the place’s own records. You choose the place (if you have several) and, if you like, a plate; “Note the car” adds it under “Inside without Stavia”.
  4. Shift — who is on shift now. “Start my shift” starts yours with one tap: the place’s notifications come to you, and if the last shift left a note you read it first (“I have read it”). “End my shift” shows how many cars you let in and out and how many problems there were, and asks for a note for the next shift.
  5. Needs you — requests to answer (with the time to answer by; only for those who manage bookings, not the entrance), problems support has not resolved yet, and new messages from drivers. A tap opens the booking or the conversation.

Further down:

  • Still inside after the end — cars still there after the booking and its grace time: how many minutes, and what it costs so far. For a private space, and at a place with many spaces, “My space is taken” sends a report to support.
  • Now — who is inside: “Inside since …”, or “Started · not checked in” when the time has started and the car is not in yet.
  • Inside without Stavia — the cars noted with “+1 car without Stavia”. “It left” asks what was paid at the place: “Paid in cash”, “Paid on the place’s terminal” or “Nothing paid here”, and the amount. The owner, the manager and reception note a payment; the entrance only “Nothing paid here”. The money is the place’s and the receipt comes from its own till; nothing goes through Stavia.
  • Arriving today — who comes before the day ends, with the hours, or “Held free until …” for a driver on the way.
  • Ended in the last 12 hours — those who left or finished.

Each row shows the time, the driver’s name, the car’s plate and the booking’s code; a tap opens the booking.

Settings and options

No settings here. The screen refreshes itself every 15 seconds: requests run out, stays start and end with the clock. On a laptop the lists are in two columns.

States

  • Nothing waiting: “No requests, problems or messages waiting.”, “No one is inside.”, “No one else is coming today. The days ahead are under Bookings.”
  • The first booking: for a week after a new business’s first paid booking, and while it is still the only one, “Your first booking” sits under the day’s count, with its code, the place and the hours.
  • Two questions: in the Test environment, once every 90 days, the person who decides for the business sees a card with a 0–10 question and a field for one suggestion. “Not now” closes it; a person of the team reads every answer.
  • Next payout: in the Test environment the owner and the manager see “Next payout: … (test)” under the count. No real money moves.
  • At the foot it says whose the data is: in the demo, “Sample data on this device only…”; in the Test environment, on the demo’s server, shared by every device.

Questions

Why can’t I see the day’s money? Only roles with the right to the money see it: the owner and the manager. Reception and the entrance see the number of bookings.

What if I don’t answer a request in time? The request lapses: nothing is charged and the driver is offered other spaces. “Needs you” shows the time to answer by.

What is “+1 car without Stavia” for? For cars you let in yourself, without a Stavia booking: for your own records and the shift’s count. Stavia takes nothing from them.

Entry

What it is

The phone at the entrance: you scan the driver’s code or type the backup code or the car’s plate, see at once whether the car may come in, and let it in or out. The driver is told at once.

Where

Stavia Business → Entry in the bottom bar (on a laptop, in the menu on the left). “Let a car in by its plate” on Today leads here too. The owner, the manager, reception and the staff at the entrance (the “Entrance” role) work with it. Under the title is the place this phone works at.

On the screen

  1. On this phone: … — whom this phone works as; every car let in is recorded under that name.
  2. Place — which of your places the entrance works at. The owner and the manager see it when they have several places.
  3. Scan the driver’s code — opens the camera. You scan the QR code from the booking, or the driver’s “My code”.
  4. Backup code or plate — when the code will not scan: type the backup code from the driver’s ticket, the booking’s code or the car’s plate. A subscriber’s plate is recognised too.
  5. Check — checks what you typed.
  6. Coming in the next 2 hours — who is due soon. A tap on a row checks it without typing.

The answer. For a moment the whole screen turns green with “LET IN” or “LET OUT”, or red with “DO NOT LET IN”, with the car’s plate, readable from a metre away; the phone vibrates differently for yes and for no. A tap closes it sooner. Below stays a card: “Valid”, “Inside” or “Not valid”, the plate, the hours (“… · paid”), the driver’s first name and the code, and the button:

  • Let in — the car comes in: “Let in at …. The driver is told at once.”
  • Let out — the car leaves. If it leaves after the end and the grace time, the note says how many minutes and what the extra time costs; the driver pays it in the app, never at the gate.
  • Paid at the place: for a stay the driver pays at the place, the card shows “To pay at the place: …” and the buttons “Paid in cash · let out” or “Paid by card · let out”. The money is the place’s.

Further down are Inside now (with “Subscriber” for subscribers) and This phone’s log: the time, the plate, “in” or “out”, how (“QR code”, “staff”) and by whom. At the foot are “Link this phone” and “Link a staff phone”.

Settings and options

  • Coming in with a code, no booking: if the place takes it (switched on in Listing), a driver without a booking shows “My code”. After the scan it says “Waiting for the driver to confirm on their phone” with their name and car; once the driver confirms, the usual answer with “Let in” appears. “Stop waiting” breaks it off.
  • Link a staff phone (the owner and the manager): shows a link code (a sample). On the staff phone: Stavia Business → Entry → “Link this phone”, and scan the code. That phone then works at this place as the person the code names: the place’s staff member, or you if it has none.

States

  • Do not let in: “No booking with this code or plate here.”, “This booking is for another place: ….”, “Too early: the car may come in from ….”, “This booking’s time is over.”, “Not paid, cancelled or not confirmed.”, “This car has already left.”
  • A doubtful code: “This code was on screen too long ago, likely a screenshot…”, “This code was not issued by Stavia, or it has been changed. Do not let the car in.”, “This code is not signed…”
  • A code without a booking: “The driver said no.”, “The driver did not confirm within two minutes…”, “This place takes no one without a booking…”
  • No connection (in the Test environment): “No connection. Entry works from what the phone holds…”: what you do waits on the phone and goes once there is a connection.
  • Empty: “No one is due.”, “No one inside.”, “Nothing yet.”
  • In the demo “Let in” only records it in Stavia: nothing opens a barrier or a door. A code from a booking made on another phone is read from the code itself, and the check stays on this phone.

Questions

The driver has no internet and the code will not scan. Ask for the backup code from the ticket, or type the car’s plate in the field.

I let out a car that stayed past the end. Who pays? The driver, in the app, for the extra time. You take no money for it at the gate.

How does someone at the entrance work from their own phone? Invite them from Staff (in the Test environment): they sign in with their own phone and a code. Or link their phone with “Link a staff phone”. Every car let in from it is recorded under their name.

Bookings

What it is

Every booking at your places: those ahead and those from the last 30 days, as a list by day or as a month’s calendar. Here you find a booking by its code or plate and download them as a spreadsheet.

Where

Stavia Business → Bookings in the bottom bar (on a laptop, in the menu on the left). The owner, the manager and reception see it. The staff at the entrance and the accountant do not have this screen.

On the screen

  1. Calendar — switches from “List” to the month by day: under each date, how many bookings start then; today has a frame. The arrows change the month, and a tap on a day lists its bookings under the calendar.
  2. Download as a spreadsheet (CSV) — downloads the bookings shown (in the calendar, the month’s) as a spreadsheet: code, place, start, end, plate, driver, status, payment, parking, Stavia fee, paid and “Test data”. The button is greyed out when there is nothing to download.
  3. Past — in the list, switches from “Upcoming” (soonest first) to past ones (latest first). Past ones go back 30 days.
  4. Code or plate — searches by the booking’s code or the plate; Cyrillic and Latin letters count alike.
  5. Place — with several places, shows one of them or “All places”.
  6. Requests — one of the filters by how a booking stands: “All”, “Requests”, “Confirmed”, “Inside”, “Ended”, “Cancelled”.

Below them the bookings are grouped by day (“Today · …” for today) with the day’s count. Each row shows the time, the driver’s name, the car’s plate, the hours (or “Request · answer by …”), the code and how it stands, for example “Confirmed”, “Inside”, “Needs your answer” or “On the way”. A tap opens the booking.

Settings and options

  • View: “List” or “Calendar”.
  • Which: “Upcoming” or “Past” (in the list only).
  • Filters: place, status, code or plate; they also apply to the spreadsheet you download.

Nothing is changed here: you confirm, let in and message the driver from the booking itself.

States

  • Nothing for the choice: “No bookings for this choice.” Change the filter or the search.
  • Past: under the list it says “These are the last 30 days.”
  • Payments a driver has not finished or that failed, holds that ran out and requests no one answered in time are not listed.
  • In the demo the bookings are sample data and stay on this device. In the Test environment you also see bookings made in the test driver app.

Questions

How do I find a booking when the driver is at the entrance? Type the car’s plate or the code in the search. To let the car in, Entry is quicker: there you let it in by its plate directly.

Can I see bookings older than 30 days? Not on this screen. In the Test environment Money shows each of the last 12 months, and Reports goes back up to 90 days.

What is the spreadsheet? A CSV file that opens in Excel or another spreadsheet editor. Every row says “Test data: yes”, because everything in the demo is sample data.

Booking

What it is

One booking up close: who is coming, with which car and when, and how the booking, the payment and the access stand. From here you answer a request, let the car in and out, message the driver and report a problem.

Where

It opens when you tap a booking in Today or Bookings. The title is “Booking” with its code. Everyone who reaches those screens opens it: the owner, the manager, reception and the staff at the entrance.

On the screen

At the top is how it stands, with an icon, for example “Confirmed”, “Inside”, “Needs your answer” or “Lapsed”. Under it is the place, tagged “SAMPLE” or “TEST”.

  1. Driver — the driver’s first name (no surname), then “Vehicle” (the plate) and “Time”: the hours, or “From … · until the driver ends it” for parking now. Once paid, “Paid by the driver” is here too.
  2. Booking — the three statuses apart: “Booking”, “Payment” (for example “Paid (test, no money moved)”) and “Access” (for example “Instructions ready” or “Entry confirmed”).
  3. Getting in — when the car came in and left: “Checked in at …”, “Checked out at …”. If it is too early or the time is over, it says why, for example “Too early: the car may come in from …”.
  4. I’ve opened it — notes that you let the car in. At a place where the host opens the door, the button is “I’ve opened it”; elsewhere it is “Let in”. Once the car is inside it becomes “Mark as left”. The driver is told at once. The button only records it in Stavia: it opens no barrier or door.
  5. Message … — opens the conversation with the driver; the number on the icon is the unread messages.
  6. Problems — the help requests about this booking: what (“I can’t get in”, “Someone is in my space”, “I’m running late”…), how far support has got and when. Under them, “Report a problem to support” opens a field, “What happened?”; “Send to support” sends it with the booking.

Settings and options

No settings here. What you can do depends on the booking:

  • A request (a place that approves bookings): “… asks to book”, the hours and “Answer by … (… left)”. “Confirm” charges the driver’s test card; “Decline” charges nothing and the driver is offered other spaces. Those who manage bookings answer (the owner, the manager, reception); the entrance sees “Waiting for the host”, without the buttons.
  • A free hold (a driver on the way): no “Getting in” and no report button until the driver pays.

States

  • Too late to answer: “Too late to answer: the request has lapsed.”
  • The place taken off by support: “This place is not taking bookings right now.” “Confirm” is greyed out.
  • The conversation not open yet: “Messages with the driver open once the booking is paid.”; after the end, “This conversation is closed: the booking ended more than 24 hours ago.”
  • No problems: “No help requested.”
  • The booking is not there: “This booking is not saved on the server, so nothing was paid for it…”
  • In the demo the booking and the payment are sample data: no real money moves. In the Test environment the driver in the test app sees your check-in and your messages at once.

Questions

Why do I see only the driver’s first name? A place gets only the name and the car’s plate: as much as it needs to let the car in.

I pressed “Let in” by mistake. Once the car is inside, only “Mark as left” remains. If something is wrong, report a problem to support from the same screen.

When is the driver charged for a request? Only when you confirm. Until then nothing is charged; without an answer the request lapses.

Messages with the driver

What it is

The conversation with the driver about one booking: the entrance, running late, the car. Each conversation belongs to the booking, not to the people: you and the driver do not swap phone numbers.

Where

From the booking: Message … (with the driver’s name). A new message also reaches Today → “Needs you” as “New message”; a tap opens it. The owner, the manager, reception and the staff at the entrance can write.

On the screen

At the top is the place’s name.

  1. Booking … · sample driver — the code of the booking the conversation is about.
  2. You write as the host… — the yellow strip says who writes and where the driver sees it: in the demo, in the driver app on this device; in the Test environment, at once in their own app.
  3. Write to the driver about the entrance or a delay… — shown while there are no messages. The driver can also write first. The driver’s messages are on the left with their first name, yours on the right, each with its time. Stavia’s own lines in the middle (“Checked in …”, “Stay extended until …”, “Problem reported: …”) say what happened to the booking.
  4. The gate is open — a ready answer in one tap. There are also “Coming to open it”, “Wait at the entrance, please” and “Thank you!”; swipe the row sideways to see them.
  5. Message — the field for your own words.
  6. Send — sends the message.

Settings and options

No settings here. Once you open the conversation, the driver’s messages count as read and the count in Today goes away.

States

  • A message refused: “For everyone’s safety, links, e-mail addresses, phone, card or account numbers and payment apps are not accepted here. Stavia never asks for payment outside the app.” Take out the link or the number and send again.
  • Not open yet: “Messages with the driver open once the booking is paid.” (parking now is open from the start).
  • Closed: “This conversation is closed: the booking ended more than 24 hours ago.” The old messages stay to read.
  • The booking is not there: “This booking is not saved on the server…”
  • In the demo the driver is a sample and does not answer by itself; you see an answer if someone writes as the driver in the driver app.

Questions

Why can’t I send my phone number? Stavia lets no phone numbers, links, card or account numbers or payment apps into the conversation, so no one takes the driver or you away from Stavia. Everything about the entrance can be said in words.

Until when can I write? From the payment until 24 hours after the booking ends.

Who sees the conversation? The driver and the place’s people who work with bookings. Support sees it when it works on the booking.

Availability

What it is

When drivers can book your place: the opening hours, the week ahead day by day, and the time you have blocked for yourself.

Where

Stavia Business → More → Availability (on a laptop, in the menu on the left). The owner, the manager and reception see it. With several places, you choose the place at the top.

On the screen

  1. Opening hours — when the place takes drivers. “Open all the time” means day and night.
  2. Change — opens the hours by day: the days of the week, “From” and “To” for each window. “Add hours” gives another window. The end can be on the next day (22:00–06:00, say). “Save hours” puts them in front of drivers at once.
  3. The next 7 days — each day is a bar from midnight to midnight in half hours. The colours are explained under the bars: open, partly booked, booked, blocked, closed. On today’s bar the line shows the time now. Drag a finger along a day to mark time: “Block time for yourself” opens with that day and those hours.
  4. Block time — the same without dragging: a day (up to 14 days ahead), “From”, “To” and why. The reason (“Personal use”, “Repairs”, “Event” or your own) is seen only by you and support. “Block” takes the time off Stavia at once.

Further down are the Blocked times, each with its reason. The back-arrow button “Give this time back to drivers” opens it again.

Settings and options

  • Opening hours: one or more windows, each with its days. At least one window stays. To take the place off Stavia, ask support.
  • Spaces by schedule (only for a place with several spaces): how many spaces drivers can book by days and hours; outside them the usual number. A day with its own number (a holiday, an event) overrides the week. “Save the schedule” applies it at once.
  • Blocked time: only in the future, and only where nothing is booked yet.

States

  • Hours or a schedule refused: “These hours leave out bookings already made: …”, and for the schedule “Not saved: these bookings would be left without a space — …”. Stavia never takes away a booked space. Let those bookings pass, or ask support.
  • A block refused: “A driver has booked this time: …” (the booking’s code), “That time is over.” or “The end must come after the start.”
  • No blocked times: “No blocked times.”
  • In the demo the data is sample data and stays on this device. In the Test environment changes reach the test driver app at once.

Questions

I blocked time a driver had already booked. The block is refused and names the booking. The booked space stays the driver’s.

Why is the bar dark when I have no bookings today? Dark is booked time on one of the next days, and red is time you blocked. Compare with the legend under the bars.

Can a driver stay after closing time? That depends on the place: the listing says whether the car may stay while the place is closed. Drivers see when they must collect the car.
