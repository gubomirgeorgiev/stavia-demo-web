Help

What to do if something goes wrong.

If…

I can't get in

Open the booking → “Help” → “I can't get in”. The place and Stavia’s support see it at once.

Someone is in my space

Open the booking → “Help” → “Someone is in my space”. Don’t park in someone else’s space; support will find a solution.

I'm running late

Open the booking → “Help” → “I'm running late”, so the place knows. If you need more time, extend from the ticket.

Leaving, but no check-out came

Open the booking → “Help” → “Leaving, but no check-out came”: support sees it at once.

Pay only in the app

Stavia never asks for payment outside the app: by phone, with a link in the chat, by bank transfer or on paper at the place. Stavia’s QR codes lead only to this site (gubomirgeorgiev.github.io). If someone asks otherwise, don’t pay and write to us from the app.

Check a document

Every document from Stavia has a number and a code to check it under its QR code. You check it in the app, on the “Check a document” page.

Write to us

From the booking (“Help”) or from “Feedback” in the app; businesses from Stavia Business → “Help”. In the pilot, support will answer from 8:00 to 22:00.

A security flaw

Found a flaw? Write to us from “Feedback” in the app, without trying other people’s data. We answer and tell you what we did.
