Availability

What it is

When drivers can book your place: the opening hours, the week ahead day by day, and the time you have blocked for yourself.

Where

Stavia Business → More → Availability (on a laptop, in the menu on the left). The owner, the manager and reception see it. With several places, you choose the place at the top.

On the screen

  1. Opening hours — when the place takes drivers. “Open all the time” means day and night.
  2. Change — opens the hours by day: the days of the week, “From” and “To” for each window. “Add hours” gives another window. The end can be on the next day (22:00–06:00, say). “Save hours” puts them in front of drivers at once.
  3. The next 7 days — each day is a bar from midnight to midnight in half hours. The colours are explained under the bars: open, partly booked, booked, blocked, closed. On today’s bar the line shows the time now. Drag a finger along a day to mark time: “Block time for yourself” opens with that day and those hours.
  4. Block time — the same without dragging: a day (up to 14 days ahead), “From”, “To” and why. The reason (“Personal use”, “Repairs”, “Event” or your own) is seen only by you and support. “Block” takes the time off Stavia at once.

Further down are the Blocked times, each with its reason. The back-arrow button “Give this time back to drivers” opens it again.

Settings and options

  • Opening hours: one or more windows, each with its days. At least one window stays. To take the place off Stavia, ask support.
  • Spaces by schedule (only for a place with several spaces): how many spaces drivers can book by days and hours; outside them the usual number. A day with its own number (a holiday, an event) overrides the week. “Save the schedule” applies it at once.
  • Blocked time: only in the future, and only where nothing is booked yet.

States

  • Hours or a schedule refused: “These hours leave out bookings already made: …”, and for the schedule “Not saved: these bookings would be left without a space — …”. Stavia never takes away a booked space. Let those bookings pass, or ask support.
  • A block refused: “A driver has booked this time: …” (the booking’s code), “That time is over.” or “The end must come after the start.”
  • No blocked times: “No blocked times.”
  • In the demo the data is sample data and stays on this device. In the Test environment changes reach the test driver app at once.

Questions

I blocked time a driver had already booked. The block is refused and names the booking. The booked space stays the driver’s.

Why is the bar dark when I have no bookings today? Dark is booked time on one of the next days, and red is time you blocked. Compare with the legend under the bars.

Can a driver stay after closing time? That depends on the place: the listing says whether the car may stay while the place is closed. Drivers see when they must collect the car.
