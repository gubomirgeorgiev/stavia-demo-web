Entry

What it is

The phone at the entrance: you scan the driver’s code or type the backup code or the car’s plate, see at once whether the car may come in, and let it in or out. The driver is told at once.

Where

Stavia Business → Entry in the bottom bar (on a laptop, in the menu on the left). “Let a car in by its plate” on Today leads here too. The owner, the manager, reception and the staff at the entrance (the “Entrance” role) work with it. Under the title is the place this phone works at.

On the screen

  1. On this phone: … — whom this phone works as; every car let in is recorded under that name.
  2. Place — which of your places the entrance works at. The owner and the manager see it when they have several places.
  3. Scan the driver’s code — opens the camera. You scan the QR code from the booking, or the driver’s “My code”.
  4. Backup code or plate — when the code will not scan: type the backup code from the driver’s ticket, the booking’s code or the car’s plate. A subscriber’s plate is recognised too.
  5. Check — checks what you typed.
  6. Coming in the next 2 hours — who is due soon. A tap on a row checks it without typing.

The answer. For a moment the whole screen turns green with “LET IN” or “LET OUT”, or red with “DO NOT LET IN”, with the car’s plate, readable from a metre away; the phone vibrates differently for yes and for no. A tap closes it sooner. Below stays a card: “Valid”, “Inside” or “Not valid”, the plate, the hours (“… · paid”), the driver’s first name and the code, and the button:

  • Let in — the car comes in: “Let in at …. The driver is told at once.”
  • Let out — the car leaves. If it leaves after the end and the grace time, the note says how many minutes and what the extra time costs; the driver pays it in the app, never at the gate.
  • Paid at the place: for a stay the driver pays at the place, the card shows “To pay at the place: …” and the buttons “Paid in cash · let out” or “Paid by card · let out”. The money is the place’s.

Further down are Inside now (with “Subscriber” for subscribers) and This phone’s log: the time, the plate, “in” or “out”, how (“QR code”, “staff”) and by whom. At the foot are “Link this phone” and “Link a staff phone”.

Settings and options

  • Let in at once on a scanned code: on unless you turn it off (the phone remembers). A valid scanned code (from the ticket, from “My code” or after the driver confirms) is recorded after 5 seconds: the card says “Letting in in 5 seconds” with a bar, “Undo” and “Let in” (at once). “Undo” records nothing (“Undone: nothing was recorded.”) and the card waits for “Let in” again. A typed plate or backup code, a refusal and paying at the place at the exit always wait for staff. The driver is told only once, when the entry is recorded.
  • Coming in with a code, no booking: if the place takes it (switched on in Listing), a driver without a booking shows “My code”. After the scan it says “Waiting for the driver to confirm on their phone” with their name and car; once the driver confirms, the usual answer with “Let in” appears. “Stop waiting” breaks it off.
  • Link a staff phone (the owner and the manager): shows a link code (a sample). On the staff phone: Stavia Business → Entry → “Link this phone”, and scan the code. That phone then works at this place as the person the code names: the place’s staff member, or you if it has none.

States

  • Do not let in: “No booking with this code or plate here.”, “This booking is for another place: ….”, “Too early: the car may come in from ….”, “This booking’s time is over.”, “Not paid, cancelled or not confirmed.”, “This car has already left.”
  • A doubtful code: “This code was on screen too long ago, likely a screenshot…”, “This code was not issued by Stavia, or it has been changed. Do not let the car in.”, “This code is not signed…”
  • A code without a booking: “The driver said no.”, “The driver did not confirm within two minutes…”, “This place takes no one without a booking…”
  • No connection (in the Test environment): “No connection. Entry works from what the phone holds…”: what you do waits on the phone and goes once there is a connection.
  • Empty: “No one is due.”, “No one inside.”, “Nothing yet.”
  • In the demo “Let in” only records it in Stavia: nothing opens a barrier or a door. A code from a booking made on another phone is read from the code itself, and the check stays on this phone.

Questions

The driver has no internet and the code will not scan. Ask for the backup code from the ticket, or type the car’s plate in the field.

I let out a car that stayed past the end. Who pays? The driver, in the app, for the extra time. You take no money for it at the gate.

How does someone at the entrance work from their own phone? Invite them from Staff (in the Test environment): they sign in with their own phone and a code. Or link their phone with “Link a staff phone”. Every car let in from it is recorded under their name.
