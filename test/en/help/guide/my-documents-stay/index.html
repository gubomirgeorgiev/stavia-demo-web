Stay and documents

What it is

One stay: where and when, with which car and on which booking, what you paid line by line and what was returned, and all its documents in the order they were issued.

Where

In the Test environment: Menu → My documents → tap a stay.

On the screen

The picture shows sample data, as in the Test environment. At the top are the place with “TEST”, the day and the hours, and “Booking ST-… · car …”.

  1. Paid — what became of the payment: “Paid”, “… returned” (some of the money), “Money returned” (all of it); “Invoice” if there is an invoice.
  2. What you paid — the payment’s lines: the parking, Stavia’s fee, vouchers if any; what was returned with a minus; “In all” after what was returned. It shows only when the stay has a payment.
  3. Paid with … — which card (its last four digits) or how it was paid, and the payment’s reference.
  4. Documents, in the order issued — the confirmation, the payment document, the refund document, the invoice and the credit note, where there are any.
  5. A document — each line gives the document’s kind, “No. …” and the date, who issued it (“Issued by …”) and the amount; a refund and a credit note have a minus. Tap the line to open the document.
  6. Each document opens on its own… — what the document says, then its PDF.

Settings and options

No settings here.

States

  • No payment: a request the host has not confirmed yet has only a confirmation; “What you paid” does not show.
  • A cancelled document: its line says “CANCELLED” and it is left out of the sums.

Questions

Why are the parking and the fee apart? The place sells the parking and the fee is Stavia’s; that is why the place’s invoice is for the parking only.

Who issues the documents? Each line says who. The invoice is the place’s, issued by Stavia in its name; the invoice says so too.
