Where I am signed in

What it is

Every device signed in to your account, and signing out of all the others with one tap. The full list is for a person signed in as themselves in the Test environment; in the demo on the device the list is empty and the button is grey.

Where

In the Test environment, signed in: in Stavia Business or in the console, the line with the name at the top → Where I am signed in.

On the screen

  1. Where I am signed in — the screen’s title.
  2. Each device signed in as you… — the list under the text. Sign out of the others if one is not yours.
  3. Sign out of every other device — signs every device but this one out of the account. It is grey when you are signed in only here.

Each device is a line: “The Stavia app”, “A browser” or “Another device”, and under it “last used …”, “this device” for the one you are looking at, and “with the second factor” if it signed in with a code from an authenticator app.

Settings and options

No settings here.

States

  • Loading: a spinner until the list arrives.
  • After signing out: “Every other device has been signed out.” and the list loads again.
  • Only this device: the button is grey.

Questions

I see a device I do not know. Tap “Sign out of every other device” and change your password.

Do I sign out of this device too? No. To sign out of it as well: the line with the name at the top → “Sign out” → “Sign out on every device”.

Does changing the password do the same? Yes: after a new password every other device is signed out.
