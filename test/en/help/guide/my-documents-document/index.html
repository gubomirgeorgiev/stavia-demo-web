Document

What it is

One document as it was issued: its kind, number and date, the amount, who issued it and to whom, the lines with VAT, the total and how it was paid. Its PDF, which is the document itself, opens from here to read, print, save or share.

Where

In the Test environment: My documents → a stay → a document, or All documents → a document. A booking’s documents also open from its ticket, and in Stavia Business and the console from “Money”.

On the screen

The picture shows sample data, as in the Test environment. The title is the document’s kind (“Payment document”, “Invoice”…), with “No. …”, the date and the amount under it.

  1. TEST — the document is a sample of the test environment, not a tax document.
  2. Open the PDF — the document’s PDF, with buttons to print, save and share it.
  3. Share or save the PDF — opens the phone’s share menu with the PDF file (Stavia-…pdf): a mail or messaging app, Drive, “Print” (and from there “Save as PDF”) — what is there depends on the phone; in a browser the file downloads.
  4. Seller — who sells: the place, with its UIC, VAT number and address. On an invoice Stavia issues in the place’s name, under the parties it says “Issued by … in the supplier’s name.”
  5. Platform — Stavia (on an invoice: Buyer — you or your company).

Further down: the place, the dates, the booking and the car; the lines (parking, Stavia’s fee) with VAT; “Total”; how it was paid; “TEST: a sample made from test data; not a tax document.”; Check a document (opens the check with this document’s code) and “The issued document is the PDF; this page shows what it says.”

Settings and options

No settings here.

States

  • Cancelled: “CANCELLED” under the number.
  • Not shared: “The PDF was not shared. Open it and use its own buttons to print, save or share.”

Questions

Which is the document — this page or the PDF? The PDF. The page shows what it says, so it reads easily on a phone.

How do I keep the document on my phone? “Share or save the PDF” → “Print” → “Save as PDF” (or Drive, or “Files” where the phone offers it). Or “Open the PDF” → the print button → “Save as PDF”.
