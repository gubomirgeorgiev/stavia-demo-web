My documents

What it is

Everything the server has issued you for your stays, laid out by stay: where and when you parked, what you paid and what was returned, and each stay’s documents — confirmation, payment document, refund document, invoice, credit note, complaint received. The screen is for the Test environment: in the demo on the device there is no server and the list is empty.

Where

In the Test environment: Menu → My documents. In the demo on the device the menu has “Invoices” in this place.

On the screen

The picture shows sample data, as in the Test environment.

  1. Stays — your stays, newest first, by month. Beside it is All documents: every document on its own line, also by month, with the filters “All”, “Invoices”, “Payments” and “Bookings”.
  2. Place, number or booking — search: by the place’s name (in Bulgarian or English), a document’s number or the booking’s reference (ST-…).
  3. With an invoice — only the stays with an invoice. Beside it: All and With a refund.
  4. A stay — the place, the day and the hours, what became of the payment (“Paid”, “€4.00 returned”, “Money returned”, “Invoice”) and how many documents it has; on the right, what you paid in all, after what was returned. Tap the line to open the stay.
  5. Check a document — under the list: opens the check, where a document’s code tells you whether the server issued it and whether it still holds.
  6. Issued by the server of the test environment… — the documents are samples, not tax documents; each has a code anyone can check it by.

A document of no booking has a line of its own and opens directly.

Settings and options

  • View: “Stays” or “All documents”.
  • Search: a place, a document’s number or a booking.
  • Filter: for stays, “All”, “With an invoice”, “With a refund”; for all documents, “All”, “Invoices”, “Payments”, “Bookings”.
  • Refresh: pull the list down.

States

  • Loading: grey lines where the documents will be.
  • Empty: “No documents yet. Each booking and payment brings its documents here.” With a filter: “None of this kind yet.” A search that finds nothing: “Nothing matches the search.”
  • Error: “This did not load” — “Nothing was lost. Try again in a moment.” and “Try again”.

Questions

Are the invoices real? No. The server of the test environment issues them; no real money moves and the documents are not tax documents.

How do I download a document? Open the stay, then the document: “Open the PDF” and “Share or save the PDF” are at the top.

How does someone check a document of mine? Every PDF has a QR code and a check code. The “Check a document” page says whether the server issued it and whether it is still valid.

Why is “My documents” missing from the menu in the demo? The server issues the documents. On a device without a server the sample invoices are under “Invoices”.
