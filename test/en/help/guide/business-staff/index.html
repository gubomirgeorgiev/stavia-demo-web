Staff

What it is

Your business’s people: who they are, in which role and for which places, the invitations for new people, the shift schedule, and the log of who let whom in. It is not a payroll and it rates no one: the log shows actions.

Where

Stavia Business → More → Staff (on a laptop, in the menu on the left). The owner and the manager see it.

On the screen

At the top are three parts: “People”, “Shifts” and “Who let whom in”.

  1. People — the business’s people now, with their count: the name, the role (“Owner”, “Manager”, “Reception”, “Entrance”, “Accountant” or an own role), for which places (“every place” or listed) and until when, if the access is for a while. “Stop access” stops it at once, on every device; the log of what the person did stays.
  2. Shifts — the schedule by place for “This week” and “Next week”. “Add a shift” on a place: who, which days, “From”, “To” and from which date; “Put on the schedule”, and it repeats every week. A tap on a shift gives “… is off that day” or “Take this shift off the schedule”. Under the schedule are the “Notes between shifts”, and “Download the shifts worked (CSV)” downloads a spreadsheet for the accountant.
  3. Who let whom in — every car let in or out in the last 30 days: by whom, how (“QR code”, “staff”) and when. You search by “Plate” and by “Who”.
  4. Sample data on this device only… — whose the data is: in the demo, this device’s only; in the Test environment, on the demo’s server.

Settings and options

  • Invite (in the Test environment): “A new invitation” with “Their name”, a “Role” (“Entrance”, “Reception”, “Manager” or “Accountant”) and “Phone or e-mail”. The entrance and reception sign in with a phone, managers and accountants with an e-mail. “Send the invitation”; invitations still waiting are above the form, each with “Cancel the invitation”. In Test the invitation is in the Test inbox.
  • Who can stop whom: the owner, anyone else; a manager, only people with no more rights than their own and not an owner. No one stops their own access.
  • Shifts track no location: whoever starts a shift on Today gets the place’s notifications.

States

  • Stopping access: “Stop …’s access?” — “At once, on every device: they can no longer let cars in or see the business. The log of what they did stays.”; then “… no longer has access.”
  • Empty: “No notes yet.”, “No one let in or out for this choice.”; for a new shift with no one to choose, “No one works at this place’s entrance yet: invite someone under People.”
  • An invitation: “Invited: they sign in with that phone or e-mail.”; refused, “This phone or e-mail is already invited.”
  • In the demo on the device there are no invitations: the people are the business’s sample ones.

Questions

Someone left. How do I stop their access? People → “Stop access” by their name. It stops at once on all their devices, and the log of who let whom in stays true.

Can staff use my password? They should not have to: each signs in with their own phone or e-mail and a code, so the log shows who did what.

How do I give the accountant the shifts for pay? Shifts → “Download the shifts worked (CSV)”: person, place, start, end and hours.
