Messages with the host

What it is

The conversation with the host about one booking: the entrance, running late, or anything else. Either of you can write first. The conversation belongs to the booking, not to the people: phones and links are not sent in it.

Where

In the booking: Messages with the host (the number on the button is the unread replies). It also opens from the notification when the host writes to you. The host answers from Stavia Business (see Messages with the driver).

On the screen

  1. Booking … · sample host — under the place’s name: which booking the conversation is about.
  2. Sample conversation: in the demo the host answers by itself after a few seconds. — the yellow strip. Once the host writes from Stavia Business on this device, it says “Sample conversation: the host writes from Stavia Business on this device.” and the sample answers stop.
  3. Write to the host about the entrance, running late or anything else. … — while the conversation is empty. Later the messages are here: yours on the right, the host’s on the left with “Host” and the time, and Stavia’s notes in the middle (“Checked in (…)”, “Stay extended until …”, “Problem reported: …”).
  4. I’m ~10 min late — ready replies sent with one tap; beside it are “Where exactly is the entrance?”, “I’ve arrived” and “Thanks!”.
  5. Message — the field for your own words.
  6. Send — sends what you wrote.

The first time, the app asks whether it may show notifications, so an answer reaches you even when you are not in the conversation.

Settings and options

No settings here.

States

  • Before paying: “The conversation with the host opens once the booking is paid.” (a “Park now” stay can talk at once).
  • A closed conversation: “This conversation is closed: the booking ended more than 24 hours ago.” The old messages stay to read.
  • Unsafe text: “For your safety, links, phone, card or account numbers and payment apps are not sent in the chat. Stavia never asks you to pay outside the app.”
  • In the Test environment: a person answers from Stavia Business, not a sample host: “Sample conversation: the host writes from Stavia Business.”

Questions

Who answers me? In the demo on the device, the sample host, by itself, after a few seconds and by key words (the entrance, running late, arriving). In the Test environment, a person answering as the host in Stavia Business.

Why didn’t my message send? It has a link, a phone, a card or account number or a payment app. Take them out and send it again.

Until when can I write? From the payment until 24 hours after the booking ends.
