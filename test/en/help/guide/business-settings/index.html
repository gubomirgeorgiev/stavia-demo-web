Settings

What it is

Your business’s details in one place: the company, its check and bank account, invoicing and DAC7, who gets reports by e-mail, the terms you accepted, and how Stavia orders places for drivers.

Where

Stavia Business → More → Settings (on a laptop, in the menu on the left). The owner and the manager see them. Under the title is the business’s name.

On the screen

The picture shows sample data, as in the Test environment.

  1. This business is part of the test world… — the sample businesses have no company details to change. For a business registered in the Test environment, here are the cards “1. The company” (name, UIC, VAT, address, “Contact: …”), “2. Check and bank account”, “4. Invoicing” and “3. Data for DAC7” (“Cadastral identifier for … of … places”), each with “Change”, which opens the same page as in the registration.
  2. Reports by e-mail — which of the people who may see the money or the reports gets which report.
  3. Daily in the evening, weekly on Monday morning… — when each report comes. Under it is each person with their role and “Daily”, “Weekly”, “Monthly”: a tap turns that report on or off for them. With no one chosen, the weekly report goes to the business’s contact.
  4. Terms for businesses — when and which version you accepted (“Accepted: …, …”), or “Not accepted yet (step 1 of the registration).”
  5. Read the terms — opens the terms for businesses.
  6. How places are shown — how Stavia orders places for drivers: by price or walking time, as the driver chooses; in the pilot no place pays to be shown higher, and ratings do not change the order.

Settings and options

  • The company’s details, the account, invoicing and DAC7 are changed through “Change”, on the registration’s pages. A checked company’s UIC does not change.
  • Reports by e-mail: for each person “Daily”, “Weekly”, “Monthly”; saved at once. In the test environments the e-mails are not sent but go to the console’s “Test inbox”.

States

  • Not saved: “Not saved: no connection, or no right to change the settings.”
  • In the demo on the device the company’s details and the reports by e-mail are not there: at the top is “This business is part of the test world: its company details are not changed here.”, and under “Reports by e-mail”, “The reports by e-mail are sent by the shared server: this version keeps everything on the phone.”

Questions

How do I change the bank account? The account is entered with the check: “2. Check and bank account” → “Change”, while the check has not started or has failed. A checked account is not changed here: write to support from Help.

Who can get reports by e-mail? Only people who may see the money or the reports: the owner, the manager, the accountant, and an own role with either of those rights.

Why is my place not shown above the others? The order follows the driver’s choice: price or walking time. In the pilot no one pays for a place at the top.
