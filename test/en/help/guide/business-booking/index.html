Booking

What it is

One booking up close: who is coming, with which car and when, and how the booking, the payment and the access stand. From here you answer a request, let the car in and out, message the driver and report a problem.

Where

It opens when you tap a booking in Today or Bookings. The title is “Booking” with its code. Everyone who reaches those screens opens it: the owner, the manager, reception and the staff at the entrance.

On the screen

At the top is how it stands, with an icon, for example “Confirmed”, “Inside”, “Needs your answer” or “Lapsed”. Under it is the place, tagged “SAMPLE” or “TEST”.

  1. Driver — the driver’s first name (no surname), then “Vehicle” (the plate) and “Time”: the hours, or “From … · until the driver ends it” for parking now. Once paid, “Paid by the driver” is here too.
  2. Booking — the three statuses apart: “Booking”, “Payment” (for example “Paid (test, no money moved)”) and “Access” (for example “Instructions ready” or “Entry confirmed”).
  3. Getting in — when the car came in and left: “Checked in at …”, “Checked out at …”. If it is too early or the time is over, it says why, for example “Too early: the car may come in from …”.
  4. I’ve opened it — notes that you let the car in. At a place where the host opens the door, the button is “I’ve opened it”; elsewhere it is “Let in”. Once the car is inside it becomes “Mark as left”. The driver is told at once. The button only records it in Stavia: it opens no barrier or door.
  5. Message … — opens the conversation with the driver; the number on the icon is the unread messages.
  6. Problems — the help requests about this booking: what (“I can’t get in”, “Someone is in my space”, “I’m running late”…), how far support has got and when. Under them, “Report a problem to support” opens a field, “What happened?”; “Send to support” sends it with the booking.

Settings and options

No settings here. What you can do depends on the booking:

  • A request (a place that approves bookings): “… asks to book”, the hours and “Answer by … (… left)”. “Confirm” charges the driver’s test card; “Decline” charges nothing and the driver is offered other spaces. Those who manage bookings answer (the owner, the manager, reception); the entrance sees “Waiting for the host”, without the buttons.
  • A free hold (a driver on the way): no “Getting in” and no report button until the driver pays.

States

  • Too late to answer: “Too late to answer: the request has lapsed.”
  • The place taken off by support: “This place is not taking bookings right now.” “Confirm” is greyed out.
  • The conversation not open yet: “Messages with the driver open once the booking is paid.”; after the end, “This conversation is closed: the booking ended more than 24 hours ago.”
  • No problems: “No help requested.”
  • The booking is not there: “This booking is not saved on the server, so nothing was paid for it…”
  • In the demo the booking and the payment are sample data: no real money moves. In the Test environment the driver in the test app sees your check-in and your messages at once.

Questions

Why do I see only the driver’s first name? A place gets only the name and the car’s plate: as much as it needs to let the car in.

I pressed “Let in” by mistake. Once the car is inside, only “Mark as left” remains. If something is wrong, report a problem to support from the same screen.

When is the driver charged for a request? Only when you confirm. Until then nothing is charged; without an answer the request lapses.
