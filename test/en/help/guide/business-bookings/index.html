Bookings

What it is

Every booking at your places: those ahead and those from the last 30 days, as a list by day or as a month’s calendar. Here you find a booking by its code or plate and download them as a spreadsheet.

Where

Stavia Business → Bookings in the bottom bar (on a laptop, in the menu on the left). The owner, the manager and reception see it. The staff at the entrance and the accountant do not have this screen.

On the screen

  1. Calendar — switches from “List” to the month by day: under each date, how many bookings start then; today has a frame. The arrows change the month, and a tap on a day lists its bookings under the calendar.
  2. Download as a spreadsheet (CSV) — downloads the bookings shown (in the calendar, the month’s) as a spreadsheet: code, place, start, end, plate, driver, status, payment, parking, Stavia fee, paid and “Test data”. The button is greyed out when there is nothing to download.
  3. Past — in the list, switches from “Upcoming” (soonest first) to past ones (latest first). Past ones go back 30 days.
  4. Code or plate — searches by the booking’s code or the plate; Cyrillic and Latin letters count alike.
  5. Place — with several places, shows one of them or “All places”.
  6. Requests — one of the filters by how a booking stands: “All”, “Requests”, “Confirmed”, “Inside”, “Ended”, “Cancelled”.

Below them the bookings are grouped by day (“Today · …” for today) with the day’s count. Each row shows the time, the driver’s name, the car’s plate, the hours (or “Request · answer by …”), the code and how it stands, for example “Confirmed”, “Inside”, “Needs your answer” or “On the way”. A tap opens the booking.

Settings and options

  • View: “List” or “Calendar”.
  • Which: “Upcoming” or “Past” (in the list only).
  • Filters: place, status, code or plate; they also apply to the spreadsheet you download.

Nothing is changed here: you confirm, let in and message the driver from the booking itself.

States

  • Nothing for the choice: “No bookings for this choice.” Change the filter or the search.
  • Past: under the list it says “These are the last 30 days.”
  • Payments a driver has not finished or that failed, holds that ran out and requests no one answered in time are not listed.
  • In the demo the bookings are sample data and stay on this device. In the Test environment you also see bookings made in the test driver app.

Questions

How do I find a booking when the driver is at the entrance? Type the car’s plate or the code in the search. To let the car in, Entry is quicker: there you let it in by its plate directly.

Can I see bookings older than 30 days? Not on this screen. In the Test environment Money shows each of the last 12 months, and Reports goes back up to 90 days.

What is the spreadsheet? A CSV file that opens in Excel or another spreadsheet editor. Every row says “Test data: yes”, because everything in the demo is sample data.
