Plans

What it is

What every driver has, and what comes after the pilot. Nothing is bought here: the screen only shows the plans.

Where

Menu → Stavia Plus (“Soon”), or Profile → Standard.

On the screen

  1. Standard — every driver’s plan, “Free”: a 15-minute free hold where the host offers it, a sample booking fee of €0.50, and vouchers and invitations for friends.
  2. Your plan — shows that Standard is your plan now.
  3. Stavia Plus — “Soon”, “Price to be announced”: a longer free hold (25 minutes), no booking fee, bookings for later with more flexible cancellation.
  4. Monthly space subscription — “Soon”, “The host sets the price”: your space every day in the same garage or car park, for people who live or work nearby.
  5. Nothing can be bought in this demo… — plans marked “Soon” come after the pilot.

Settings and options

No settings here. The cards do not respond to a tap.

States

The screen is the same for every driver, in the demo and in the Test environment. The €0.50 fee is a sample.

Questions

How do I move to Stavia Plus? Not yet: the plan comes after the pilot. Until then everyone is on Standard.

Why do some places have no free hold? The hold depends on the host; Standard gives it only where the host offers it.

What will the monthly subscription cost? The place’s host will set the price.
