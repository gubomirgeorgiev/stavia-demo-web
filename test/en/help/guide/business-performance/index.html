How you are doing

What it is

The last 30 days of each of your places next to the average of other businesses’ places nearby: bookings, how fast you answer requests, how many bookings you cancelled, the rating, and what the listing still lacks. No other place is named.

Where

Stavia Business → More → How you are doing (on a laptop, in the menu on the left). The owner, the manager and the accountant see it; the accountant has it straight in the bottom bar.

On the screen

The picture shows sample data, as in the Test environment.

  1. The last 30 days of each of your places… — what you are compared with: the average of other businesses’ places within 2 km, when there are at least three; ratings are for the last 24 months, from 5.
  2. Nearby — each published place is a card with its name and two columns: “Yours” (your numbers) and “Nearby” (the average of the places near it). The rows are “Bookings” (how many bookings the place had), “Bookings you cancelled” (how many you cancelled) and “Rating” (your average, from 5 ratings, beside the average nearby).
  3. Answer to a request — “Median; an unanswered one counts as a day”, in minutes.
  4. The listing has all it needs. — under each card: whether the listing lacks anything.
  5. The listing still lacks: … — what to finish, for example a photo of the place or the test booking.

Settings and options

No settings here. Pull the list down to refresh it.

States

  • No comparison: “Fewer than three places of other businesses within 2 km: no comparison yet.” Then “Nearby” shows “—”.
  • No published place: “No published place yet.”
  • An error: “This did not load” with “Try again”.
  • In the demo on the device: “Worked out on the shared server: this version keeps only the phone’s own bookings.”

Questions

Which places am I compared with? Other businesses’ places within 2 km, only when there are at least three, and only as an average: none is named.

Why is my answer time slow when I answer quickly? A request with no answer counts as a whole day. Answer every one, with “Confirm” or “Decline”.

What if the listing lacks something? Finish it: the photos and the test booking are in the registration, the prices and rules in Listing.
