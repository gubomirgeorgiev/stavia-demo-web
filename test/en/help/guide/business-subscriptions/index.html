Subscriptions

What it is

Your place’s monthly cards for residents and companies, paid to the place outside Stavia. You write them here so the same spaces are not sold twice, and so Entry knows the subscribers’ cars by their plates.

Where

Stavia Business → More → Subscriptions (on a laptop, in the menu on the left). The owner, the manager and reception see it.

On the screen

  1. Monthly cards for residents and companies… — what subscriptions are for; the “SAMPLE” tag says everything is a sample.
  2. Add a subscription — a new one: the place (if you have several), “Who holds it (e.g. Flat 5, a company)”, “Plates, separated by commas (up to 5)”, “Spaces”, “From” (“Today” or “The 1st of next month”), “For how long” (1, 3, 6 or 12 months) and “Price a month (optional)”. “Save the subscription” saves it.
  3. No subscriptions at this place. — each place is a card of its own, with the number of current subscriptions. Each subscription shows who holds it, the plates, the spaces, the dates and the price “a month, paid at the place”. The menu “What to do with this subscription” gives “Renew” and “End”.
  4. Sample data on this device only… — whose the data is.

For a place with several spaces, above the subscriptions it says “Spaces: subscribers … · for Stavia up to … · at the place …”. Under the current ones are those Ended or expired, each with “Ended” or “Expired”; an expired one can still be renewed.

Settings and options

  • Renew: “For how long” and the new dates; it goes on from its last day (or from today if it has expired): “Renewed until …”.
  • End: “End …’s subscription now? Entry will no longer let their cars in as subscribers.” An ended subscription stays in the list as it was.

States

  • Not enough spaces: “… spaces short if every subscriber comes and all of Stavia’s spaces are booked. Lower Stavia’s spaces in Availability, or the subscriptions.”
  • Refused: “Write who holds it and at least one plate (up to 5).”, “Write the price in euros, from 0 to 10,000.”, “It was not saved. Check the connection and try again.”
  • Saved: “The subscription is saved.”
  • The money for a subscription does not go through Stavia: it is paid at the place. In the demo the subscriptions stay on this device.

Questions

How does Entry let a subscriber in? Type the car’s plate in “Backup code or plate”: the answer is “Valid” with the holder and the date the subscription runs to. Once in, the car is under “Inside now” with “Subscriber”.

Does Stavia take a commission on subscriptions? No. Subscriptions are paid to the place outside Stavia; they are only written down here.

My subscribers use spaces Stavia offers. Lower Stavia’s spaces in Availability, or the subscriptions: the warning says how many spaces would be short.
