A place from its link

What it is

A place opened from its own link or QR code. The app opens the map with the place’s space over it, as if you had tapped it on the map: you see the price and how you get in, and can hold it, book it or park straight away.

Where

The place’s QR code leads to its page on Stavia’s site. In the Sandbox environment that page has a “Book on Stavia” button, which opens this link in the web version of the app. In the Test environment the site says instead to open the app and search for the place by name. The demo on the device has no site.

On the screen

  1. SAMPLE — the place’s tag (“TEST” for a real place in the test data), beside its name and address.
  2. Guaranteed space — the space waits for you once you hold or book it; beside it is what the place has, and whether it is open now.
  3. Hold free · 15 min — the main action for “Now” (for “Later”, “Reserve · …”; see The free hold and Payment).
  4. Park now · pay when you leave — park straight away and pay when you leave (see Park now, pay when you leave).

The rest is as in any space: the price, getting in, the opening hours, the entrance, the ratings, “Report this place” (see A space).

Settings and options

No settings here.

States

  • The place is not there: “This place is not on Stavia or is no longer published.” → “Find a space” opens the map.
  • Opening: “Opening the place…” for a moment, until the map opens.
  • A place taken down by support, or one without a listing, also leads to “This place is not on Stavia or is no longer published.”

Questions

Is it different from finding the place on the map? Not for you: the same space, the same prices and rules. For the place, a booking made from here comes from its own channel.

The link says the place is not on Stavia. The place was taken down or is not published yet. Tap “Find a space” and look nearby.

Why is the map somewhere else, not where I am? The map moves to the entrance of the place in the link, so you see it. Close the space to go back to the list.
