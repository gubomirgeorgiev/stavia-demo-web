Park now, pay when you leave

What it is

Park straight away without choosing until when, and when you leave pay for exactly the time you parked, at the space’s price. No deposit. The payment is a test: no real money moves.

Where

In a space on the map, with “Now”: Park now · pay when you leave under the main button. The same sheet also opens on your phone when staff at an entrance scan My code; then its title is “Coming in with your code”, and “Not me” says no.

On the screen

  1. Park now, pay when you leave — the title, with the place’s name under it. The cross closes the sheet and nothing starts.
  2. You can stay until — the latest time: until the space’s next booking, until the place closes, or 24 hours. Above it, “Price”: per hour and the most for a day.
  3. No deposit. The test card is charged when you end the stay — … — how you pay. Under it is the test card (“Test card … · …”) with “Change” to choose another.
  4. I ask for the parking to start at its time and know that the 14 days to withdraw then end. — the tick without which the stay does not start. It is never ticked in advance. Under it: “An order with an obligation to pay”.
  5. Start parking — starts the stay and opens the booking: the amount so far, until when you can stay, and “End and pay”.

Where the place takes money itself (cash, say), you choose above the text between “In the app: the test card, when you leave” and “At the place: …, when you leave”. At the place you pay the staff when you leave, and Stavia charges nothing to your card.

Settings and options

  • How you pay: with the test card in the app or, where the place takes it, at the place.
  • Card: “Change” picks another test card; with no card the button is “Add a test card”.

States

  • An unpaid stay: “You have a stay whose payment failed. Pay for it to park this way again.” → “Go to the unpaid stay”.
  • Already parked this way: “You already have a running or unpaid stay.”
  • A grey button: the tick is not set, or the space is not free even for the shortest stay.
  • Someone took it just before you: “Someone just took this space”; nothing was charged.
  • In the demo the car goes into no real car park, and no real money moves.

Questions

How much will I pay? The time you parked at the space’s price, at most the daily maximum, but at least the space’s shortest stay (1 hour in the samples). Paying in the app adds Stavia’s sample fee. The amount so far shows in the booking.

How do I end it? In the booking: “End and pay”. If you pay at the place, the staff end the stay at the exit.

What happens after “You can stay until”? The car must be out by then. In the demo a stay of this kind lasts 24 hours at most.
