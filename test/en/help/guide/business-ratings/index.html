Ratings

What it is

What drivers say about your places: each place’s average, the four questions, every rating (newest first) with the reasons behind a low one, and one public answer from you to each.

Where

Stavia Business → More → Ratings (on a laptop, in the menu on the left). The owner and the manager see them and answer.

On the screen

  1. Ratings — a card for each place with its number of ratings. At the top are the average with stars and “… ratings · the last 24 months”, then what share of drivers (in per cent) said yes to each question (“The entrance was easy to find”, “The instructions for getting in were right”, “The space was free and as described”, “The car was safe”) and how many ratings have 5, 4, 3, 2 and 1 stars.
  2. Ratings from confirmed stays only… — you do not see who wrote one or which booking it is; a low rating (3 stars or fewer) reaches the business by e-mail at once. In the Test environment no real e-mail is sent: it is in the Test inbox in the console; in the demo on the device there is none.
  3. Answer — a public answer to one rating. It opens “A public answer” with the rules: to the point, without the driver’s details (date, plate, the booking) and without offers. “Publish the answer” shows it with the rating as “Answer from …”.

Each rating shows the stars, the driver’s first name and initial, the month of the stay, “Confirmed stay” and the words, if any. Under a low rating are the reasons the driver chose, for example “Hard to find the entrance”, “The code or barrier did not work”, “The space was taken”, “The staff did not help”. At the foot, “How ratings work” explains who rates, that every rating is published, when one is taken down, that there are no rewards, how the average is worked out, and that ratings do not rank places.

Settings and options

No settings here. Each rating has one answer; once you publish it, the “Answer” button goes. If the driver changes the rating after your answer, it says “The driver changed the rating after your answer.”, in public your answer is marked as one to an earlier version, you get an e-mail, and you may answer again (“Answer again”). You may not ask a driver to change a rating. Pull the list down to refresh it.

States

  • Fewer than 5 ratings: “New in Stavia: not enough ratings yet. The average shows from 5.”
  • No ratings: “No ratings of this place yet.”
  • Stavia’s problem: “Stavia’s note: the problem was ours, not the place’s; not counted in the average.”
  • The answer is published: “The answer is published; the driver sees it in the app.” Refused: “No links, phones, e-mails, plates or personal ID numbers in an answer.”
  • In the demo the ratings are samples and stay on this device.

Questions

Can I take a bad rating down? No. Stavia takes a rating down only for illegal content, another person’s data, spam and the like, never for a low rating, criticism or the place disagreeing; a person decides. You can answer it publicly, to the point.

Do ratings change how high my place is shown? No. Drivers order places by price or walking time; ratings do not change the order.

Why can’t I see who wrote it? That is so for every rating: a place does not see who wrote it or which booking it is. You see the first name and initial and the month of the stay, as everyone does, never the plate, the day or the hour.
