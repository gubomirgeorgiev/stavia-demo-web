Ratings from confirmed stays

What it is

All of a place’s ratings from the last 24 months: the average, the answers to the four questions, how many there are of each star, and the reviews themselves. Only drivers with a confirmed stay there rate it. The title at the top is the place’s name.

Where

In a space on the map: the star line under the name (the average and the number of ratings), or “All … ratings” under “Ratings from confirmed stays”. The average and the star line show from 5 ratings.

On the screen

  1. Ratings from confirmed stays — under it the average in a large figure with stars, how many ratings there are for the last 24 months, then the share of “yes” for each question (“The entrance was easy to find”, “The instructions for getting in were right”, “The space was free and as described”, “The car was safe”) and how many ratings there are of each star.
  2. Newest — the reviews by date, newest first.
  3. Lowest — the lowest ratings first.
  4. More — a review’s “…” menu: “Report” (not on your own reviews).

Each review shows the stars, the author’s first name and initial, the month of the stay, “Confirmed stay”, the text if there is one, and the place’s answer (“Answer from …”). Sample reviews carry “SAMPLE”. At the bottom, “How ratings work” explains who rates and how we check, that low and high ratings alike are published, when we take one down, that there are no rewards, how the average is worked out and what is shown about the author.

Settings and options

  • Order: “Newest” or “Lowest”.

States

  • Under 5 ratings: “New in Stavia: not enough ratings yet. The average shows from 5.”
  • A report: in a test environment, a reason and a few words → “Send the report”; “Thank you. Support will look within 72 hours.” A report alone takes nothing down. In the demo on this device it only says that the report reaches no one.
  • A problem of Stavia’s: “Stavia’s note: the problem was ours, not the place’s; not counted in the average. …”
  • Real places in the test data (“TEST”) are not rated in Stavia’s tests.

Questions

Who can rate? Only the driver who booked, after the visit is confirmed: a paid stay, the code read at the entrance, or the arrival. Within 14 days of the stay, and one rating per driver and place, which they change at any time; a change says “changed on …”, and an answer from the place before it says “Answer from … to an earlier version”.

Does Stavia take bad ratings down? Not for a low rating, criticism or the place disagreeing. Only for illegal content, personal data, spam and the like; a person decides, and the author can contest it.

Do ratings change the order of the spaces? No. In the pilot places are listed by distance, a free space and price.
