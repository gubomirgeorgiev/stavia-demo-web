More

What it is

On a phone the bottom bar holds five parts at most. When your role opens more, the bar keeps Today, Bookings, Entry and Places (those of them your role opens), and the others are here, under More.

Where

Stavia Business on a phone → More (last in the bottom bar). It is there only when you have more than five parts, as the owner, the manager and reception do. The staff at the entrance and the accountant have everything in the bar and no More. On a laptop there is no More: the menu on the left shows every part.

On the screen

One list; each row opens its part. The picture shows the owner’s list.

  1. Availability — opening hours, the week ahead and blocked time.
  2. Money — the payouts, the month and the documents.
  3. Staff — the people, the roles and the shifts.
  4. Reports — revenue, bookings, occupancy and rating for a period, against the one before.
  5. Ratings — drivers’ ratings and your answers.
  6. Help — the conversation with Stavia’s support.

Among them are also How you are doing, Subscriptions and Settings.

Settings and options

No settings here. What the list holds depends on the role:

  • Owner and manager: Availability, Money, Staff, Reports, Ratings, How you are doing, Subscriptions, Settings, Help.
  • Reception: Availability, Subscriptions, Help.
  • An own role: the parts its rights allow.

States

  • A business with no place yet opens on Registration instead.

Questions

I cannot see Money in the list. Your role does not open the money: it is for the owner, the manager and the accountant. The owner or the manager gives roles, with an invitation from Staff (in the Test environment).

Why is there no More on the laptop? There the menu on the left fits every part.
