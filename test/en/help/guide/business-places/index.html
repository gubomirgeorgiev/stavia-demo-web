Places

What it is

Every place of your business, each with how it stands on Stavia, and the way to its listing. From here you go on with a new place’s registration and show drivers the place’s page and QR code.

Where

Stavia Business → Places in the bottom bar (on a laptop, in the menu on the left). The owner and the manager see it. Under the title is the number of places.

On the screen

Each place is a card with its name, its address, and how many spaces it offers at what price an hour.

  1. Published — how the place stands: “Published” (drivers see it), “In review” (Stavia is checking it), “Draft” (not sent yet) or “Suspended” (support has taken it off Stavia).
  2. Listing — opens the place’s listing: the price, the rules, how drivers get in, the photos.
  3. Sample data on this device only… — whose the data is: in the demo, this device’s only; in the Test environment, on the demo’s server.

Settings and options

  • Continue the registration — for a place not published yet: opens this place’s registration, with the steps done and those still to do.
  • Page and QR — in the Test environment, for a published place that is not suspended: “The place’s page” with its QR code, its address and the tag “TEST · Stavia’s test site, not the real one.” Drivers open the page with the code: the price, the hours, the entrance and whether there is a space now, and they book from it. The buttons: “Copy the link”, “Send”, “Open” and “Sign to print”.
  • Sign to print — an A4 sheet for the entrance, in Bulgarian and English: “Park here with Stavia”, the place’s name, the QR code, two steps for the driver, and a reminder that the address must begin with Stavia’s address, and if it does not, not to pay. The sign says “TEST”: no real space is behind the code. You print it or send it as a PDF.
  • Add a place — in the Test environment, for a registered business: a new place goes through the same steps as the registration. “A new place is a draft: drivers see it once Stavia has reviewed it.”

States

  • In the demo on the device there is no “Page and QR” and no “Add a place”: they work only with the server, in the Test environment.
  • A suspended place has no page: drivers cannot book it until support lets it back.

Questions

Why is my place “In review”? A person at Stavia checks every new place before drivers see it. In Registration → “9. Review by Stavia” you see when it is published, or what to fix (“Returned for changes: …”).

Where do I put the QR code? At the entrance, on the sign to print, or send the link to your drivers. The code leads to this place’s page.

How do I change the price? In the place’s Listing.
