Complaints and disputes

What it is

How you complain about a place or about Stavia, what happens to the complaint, when you get an answer, and where to go if you do not agree. The text is a draft for the lawyer, and it says so.

Where

Menu → Complaints and disputes.

On the screen

  1. DRAFT for the lawyer: not in force. In the test environments no money moves.
  2. About a booking: open it and choose Help… — so support sees everything that happened. About anything else: “Feedback” at the top of the screen. You can complain about the place or about Stavia.
  3. Every complaint is written in our register… — you get a document with its number and date in the booking’s documents. Support looks at it, asks the place if needed and answers you in the app.
  4. We answer as soon as we can and within 30 days. A problem with getting in is handled at once, while you are at the place.
  5. You can turn to the conciliation committees of the Commission for Consumer Protection (КЗП)… — they settle disputes out of court and free of charge; or you go to court.

Further down is “For businesses”: a business complains in Stavia Business → Help, and a decision about its listing comes with its reasons and can be contested. The button “The Commission for Consumer Protection (kzp.bg)” opens the commission’s site in the browser.

Settings and options

No settings here.

States

  • The screen is the same everywhere.
  • “Feedback” (the button on the right of the yellow strip at the top) is only in the Test environment. In the demo on the device a complaint about a booking is made from its ticket: “Help with this booking”.
  • The document with the complaint’s number (“Complaint received”) comes from the server, so it is in the Test environment: in “My documents” and in the booking’s documents.

Questions

Where do I write if I cannot get into the car park? On the booking’s ticket: “Help with this booking”. A problem with getting in is handled at once, while you are at the place.

How do I know my complaint was received? In the Test environment you get a “Complaint received” document with a number and a date.

What do I do if I do not agree with the answer? Turn to the КЗП’s conciliation committees (free, and out of court) or to the courts.
