Invite a friend

What it is

Two kinds of invitation: for a friend who drives, and for someone with an empty space to offer. Both codes are yours. In the demo nothing is sent and the amounts are samples.

Where

Menu → Invite a friend.

On the screen

  1. Give €5, get €5 — your friend gets €5 for their first parking and you get €5 when they sign up. Under “YOUR CODE” is your code: your first name in Latin letters and “-5”.
  2. Copy the code — copies the code for you to send yourself: “Code copied.”
  3. Recommend a car park or garage — for a host: when they list their first space on Stavia, you get €10. The code ends in “-HOST” and has its own “Copy the code”.
  4. Your invitations — how many friends have signed up and how many hosts have listed spaces.
  5. Earned: €0.00 — what your invitations have brought you.

Further down, “How it works” gives the three steps: you send your code to a friend; they sign up for Stavia (or list a space, if they are a host); you both get a parking voucher. At the bottom: “In this demo nothing is sent and the codes are samples.”

Settings and options

No settings here.

States

Both cards are labelled “SAMPLE”. The counts stay at zero and nothing is sent, in the Test environment too: the app only copies the code.

Questions

Do I get a voucher if a friend uses my code? No: invitations are not sent anywhere, the codes are samples and the counts stay at zero.

Can I type a friend’s code in “Vouchers”? No: “Add a code” takes only voucher codes, and for an invitation code in the demo it says “No such code.”

Why is my code in Latin letters? So anyone can type it, on any keyboard.
