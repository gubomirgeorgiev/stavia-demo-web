Messages with the driver

What it is

The conversation with the driver about one booking: the entrance, running late, the car. Each conversation belongs to the booking, not to the people: you and the driver do not swap phone numbers.

Where

From the booking: Message … (with the driver’s name). A new message also reaches Today → “Needs you” as “New message”; a tap opens it. The owner, the manager, reception and the staff at the entrance can write.

On the screen

At the top is the place’s name.

  1. Booking … · sample driver — the code of the booking the conversation is about.
  2. You write as the host… — the yellow strip says who writes and where the driver sees it: in the demo, in the driver app on this device; in the Test environment, at once in their own app.
  3. Write to the driver about the entrance or a delay… — shown while there are no messages. The driver can also write first. The driver’s messages are on the left with their first name, yours on the right, each with its time. Stavia’s own lines in the middle (“Checked in …”, “Stay extended until …”, “Problem reported: …”) say what happened to the booking.
  4. The gate is open — a ready answer in one tap. There are also “Coming to open it”, “Wait at the entrance, please” and “Thank you!”; swipe the row sideways to see them.
  5. Message — the field for your own words.
  6. Send — sends the message.

Settings and options

No settings here. Once you open the conversation, the driver’s messages count as read and the count in Today goes away.

States

  • A message refused: “For everyone’s safety, links, e-mail addresses, phone, card or account numbers and payment apps are not accepted here. Stavia never asks for payment outside the app.” Take out the link or the number and send again.
  • Not open yet: “Messages with the driver open once the booking is paid.” (parking now is open from the start).
  • Closed: “This conversation is closed: the booking ended more than 24 hours ago.” The old messages stay to read.
  • The booking is not there: “This booking is not saved on the server…”
  • In the demo the driver is a sample and does not answer by itself; you see an answer if someone writes as the driver in the driver app.

Questions

Why can’t I send my phone number? Stavia lets no phone numbers, links, card or account numbers or payment apps into the conversation, so no one takes the driver or you away from Stavia. Everything about the entrance can be said in words.

Until when can I write? From the payment until 24 hours after the booking ends.

Who sees the conversation? The driver and the place’s people who work with bookings. Support sees it when it works on the booking.
