Terms for drivers

What it is

The rules between you, Stavia and the places, in seven short parts: who we are, the booking and its price, cancelling, the stay, your car, complaints and your data. The text is a draft for the lawyer and is not in force.

Where

Menu → Terms for drivers. Also under the pay button (“By paying you accept the terms for drivers (a draft).”) and when you create a profile.

On the screen

  1. DRAFT for the lawyer (…): not in force. The brackets hold the text’s version. In the test environments no money moves.
  2. Stavia is a platform… (“1. Who we are”) — it shows you spaces that places offer and takes the payment for them. The parking itself is the place’s service, under its rules; Stavia’s fee is for the booking.
  3. You see the whole price for the time you chose… (“2. The booking and its price”) — with Stavia’s fee, before you pay. Once paid, the booking is confirmed.
  4. You cancel for free until the time each place states… (“3. Cancelling”) — after it the booking is not refunded. If the place cannot take you in, you get all your money back.
  5. Leave by the end of your booking, or add time in the app… (“4. The stay”) — time beyond the booking is paid at the place’s rate.

Further down: “5. Your car” (a place looks after cars only where it says it is guarded), “6. Complaints” (from the booking, with a number and a document, an answer within 30 days) and “7. Your data” (a place sees only your first name and your car’s plate; more in “Privacy”).

Settings and options

No settings here.

States

  • With the first payment on this phone the terms are accepted once: in the Test environment the server records the version and the moment. In the demo on the device there is no server and nothing is sent.
  • The text is the same everywhere.

Questions

Can I cancel for free? Until the time the place states. After it the booking is not refunded.

Who is responsible for my car? The place looks after it only if it says it is guarded. Stavia is not liable for damage, except where the law makes it liable.

What happens if I stay past the end? Time beyond the booking is paid at the place’s rate. You can add time from the ticket while the place allows it.
