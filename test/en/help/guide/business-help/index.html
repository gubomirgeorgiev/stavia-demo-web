Help

What it is

Your business’s conversation with Stavia’s support: a question about the listing, the money, the staff or the app. Here too: where a booking’s problem goes, and whether the app reaches the server.

Where

Stavia Business → More → Help (on a laptop, in the menu on the left). The staff at the entrance and the accountant have it straight in the bottom bar. Everyone at the business sees it, in any role.

On the screen

The picture shows sample data, as in the Test environment.

  1. Write to Stavia’s support — the conversation with support. The answer comes here, and an e-mail says that support has answered (in the test environments it is not sent but kept in the console’s “Test inbox”). Your messages are on the right and support’s on the left, each with “The business” or “Stavia’s support” and the time.
  2. No messages yet. — shown until you have written.
  3. Your message — the field for your words, up to 2,000 characters.
  4. Send — sends the message. In the Test environment the camera button beside it is “Add a photo of the problem”: it picks a photo from the device to go with the message; show the car and the space, no people.
  5. A problem with a booking — a problem with a particular booking is not written here: open the booking and choose “Report a problem to support”. Support sees it with the booking’s whole story. Whoever has Bookings also sees a button to it.
  6. Connected to the test environment’s server. — “The service”: whether the app reaches the server. With no connection it says “No connection to the server: what you change is sent once it is back.”, and in the demo on the device “This version keeps everything on the phone, without a server.”

Settings and options

No settings here. The conversation refreshes itself every 15 seconds while it is open.

States

  • Not sent: “Not sent: try again.”; for a photo, “The photo was not sent: try again.”
  • A photo ready to go: “A photo goes with the message. Show the car and the space, no people.”
  • In the demo the conversation stays on this device; support answers from the console. In the Test environment Stavia’s team sees it.

Questions

Where do I write if a driver cannot get in? From the booking itself: “Report a problem to support”. That way support sees the booking, the payment and the messages.

Do I have to keep the screen open to see the answer? No. The answer stays in this conversation, and an e-mail tells you it is there (in the test environments, in the “Test inbox”, with no real e-mail).

Can I change the place’s address? While the place is a draft, yourself, in the registration. Once Stavia has seen it, by asking support here: the address, the entrance and how drivers get in are then changed with Stavia.
