Reports

What it is

How your places are doing over a chosen period: revenue, bookings and occupancy, each against the period before, the drivers’ rating, and the revenue by day. On a computer also when the places are full, cancellations, stays and drivers, and a spreadsheet.

Where

Stavia Business → More → Reports (on a laptop, in the menu on the left). The owner, the manager and the accountant see them; the accountant has them straight in the bottom bar.

On the screen

The picture shows sample data, as in the Test environment.

  1. Test numbers: … — worked out from the test world’s bookings, by the same definitions as for real ones. Beside it is the “TEST” tag.
  2. This month — the period: “7 days”, “30 days” (chosen when it opens), “90 days” or “This month”.
  3. Revenue — what drivers paid for parking in the period, with “▲/▼ … against the period before”. Beside it, “Bookings”, with the same comparison.
  4. Occupancy — the hours sold against the hours offered, with the comparison. Beside it, “Rating”: the average once there are 5 ratings, otherwise “—” and “From 5 ratings”.
  5. By day — each day’s revenue as a bar; the best day is the tallest.
  6. The full reports and the spreadsheet are on a computer. — on a phone the report ends here.

At the bottom it says whose the data is: in the Test environment, “Sample data on the demo’s server: every device signed in to the demo sees it.”

On a computer, also: “When the places are full”: the days of the week by hour, darker is fuller, with the emptiest hour (“Emptiest: … Hours you could sell: a lower price or free first minutes then.”, once there is enough data) and “Download the days (CSV)”; and “Cancellations, stays and drivers”: “Cancelled and refunded”, “Paid but did not come”, “Average stay”, “Left more than 15 minutes late”, “Cars without Stavia · sold at the place” and “New drivers / returning”.

Settings and options

Only the period. Who gets reports by e-mail (daily, weekly, monthly) is chosen in Settings.

States

  • Not enough data yet: “Not enough data yet for percentages and comparisons: … more days and … more bookings in the period.” Until then occupancy shows “—” and there are no comparisons.
  • On a phone: “The full reports and the spreadsheet are on a computer.”
  • Few drivers: new and returning drivers are “Shown once there are … drivers in the period”, so no one can be recognised.
  • In the demo on the device: “Reports are worked out on the shared server: this version keeps only the phone’s own bookings.”

Questions

Why are there no percentages? A new place does not have enough days and bookings for a fair comparison yet. The note says how many more it needs.

How is occupancy worked out? The hours sold against the hours the spaces were offered to Stavia.

Are cars without Stavia in the revenue? No: they are a line of their own, “Noted by the place; not through Stavia”.
