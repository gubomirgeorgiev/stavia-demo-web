Terms for businesses

What it is

The terms between Stavia and the business, in seven short parts: who does what, the listing, money, how places are shown, suspending a listing, data, and changes. For now they are a draft for the lawyer and not in force.

Where

Stavia Business → the line with your name in the top bar → Terms for businesses. Also from the registration, step “1. The company” → “Read the terms”. Everyone at the business opens them, in any role.

On the screen

  1. DRAFT for the lawyer (…) — the terms’ version, and that they are not in force yet. In the test environments no money moves.
  2. The parties — Stavia brings drivers to your places and takes their payments on your behalf; the parking, the space, the entrance and the staff are yours.
  3. Money — Stavia keeps a commission set in the tariff you accepted (a starting rate first, with its end date) and pays out the rest monthly with a statement; the commission invoice comes by the 5th of the next month.
  4. How places are shown — drivers see the places that fit their time and car, ordered by price or walking time as they choose. No place pays to be shown higher in the pilot; ratings do not change the order.
  5. Suspending a listing — only decided by a person, with a reason stated, and you can contest it.

Further down are The listing (you set the prices, hours and rules; a change applies to new bookings), Data (you receive the driver’s first name and the car’s plate to let them in) and Changes and ending (a change is announced at least 15 days ahead; either party may end the contract with 30 days’ notice).

Settings and options

No settings here. You accept the terms in the registration, step “1. The company”: “I accept the terms for businesses” is saved with the version and the date and is not taken back there.

States

  • The terms are a draft awaiting the lawyer; the version is in the note at the top.

Questions

Do I pay to have my place shown higher? No. No place pays for that in the pilot, and ratings do not change the order.

Can Stavia suspend my listing? Only a person, with a reason they tell you, and you can contest it.

What drivers’ data do I get? Their first name and the car’s plate: as much as you need to let them in. Stavia keeps the rest, as Privacy says.
