Change the password

What it is

A new password for an account with an e-mail, with the current one to confirm. Once the password changes, every other device is signed out of the account. The screen works for a person signed in as themselves with an e-mail in the Test environment; in the demo on the device the fields show but nothing changes.

Where

In the Test environment, signed in with an e-mail: in Stavia Business or in the console, the line with the name at the top → Change the password.

On the screen

  1. Current password — the password you signed in with.
  2. New password — the new one.
  3. At least 12 characters — how long the new password must be.
  4. Change the password — changes it. Then: “The password is changed. Every other device has been signed out.” and back to where you were.

Settings and options

No other settings. The passwords are hidden while you type them.

States

  • A short new password: “The password must be at least 12 characters.”
  • A wrong current password: “The e-mail or the password is wrong.”
  • Too many tries: “Too many tries. Wait a minute and try again.”
  • No connection: “It did not work. Check the connection and try again.”

Questions

I forgot the current password. Sign out, and when signing in choose “Forgot the password”: the code comes to the e-mail (in the test environments, to the Test inbox).

Why was my phone signed out? After a password change every other device is signed out; sign in there with the new one.

I work at the entrance; do I have a password? No. Entrance staff sign in with a phone and a code, so this line is not shown to them.
