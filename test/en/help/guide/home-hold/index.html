The free hold

What it is

We keep the space just for you for up to 15 minutes while you are on the way, without paying. First you say how soon you will be there; when you are sure, you reserve and pay for the stay from that moment.

Where

In a space on the map, with “Now”: Hold free · 15 min. Only where the host offers the free hold. While it runs, a “Held until …” chip on the map opens it again.

On the screen

  1. The space waits for you until … · free — above it, the ring with the minutes and seconds left. It empties over the 15 minutes and pulses over the last two.
  2. Directions — navigation to the entrance. Beside the button are the place’s name, your plate and how you get in; under it, the description of the entrance.
  3. Reserve · pay from now — opens “Until when are you staying?”: choose how long, see the price and pay with the test card (“Test payment for … No real money is taken.”). The stay starts when you reserve, whether or not you are there yet, and the space waits for you.
  4. Entry instructions appear once you’ve paid for your stay. — the code or instructions for getting in are in the booking, after you pay.

Before the hold comes “How soon will you be there?” with a wheel from 5 minutes to 2 hours (5 and 10 minutes are grey: the shortest hold is 15 minutes). Up to 15 minutes, the wheel is followed by “Held until …” and “Free”, and the button is “Hold free”. Over 15 minutes: “You pay from now, …” — “Over 15 minutes, the stay is paid from now, even before you arrive.”; the button is “Continue” and goes straight to “Until when are you staying?”. “Cancel” goes back to the space.

Settings and options

No settings here. You choose how soon you arrive before the hold, and how long you stay when you reserve.

States

  • The hold ran out: “The hold ran out” — “The space has been released and is hidden from you until …” and “Find another space”. The space is hidden from you for 1 hour.
  • Two minutes before the end: the phone nudges you once; if notifications are allowed, “Your space waits until …” arrives, with “Reserve” and “Directions”.
  • Already holding another space: you hold one space at a time. When you take another, the app asks “Switch spaces?” (see A space).
  • Soon after a hold here ran out: the space is hidden in the list, and in the space’s panel the button is grey: “Hidden from you until …”.
  • Someone took it just before you: “Someone just took this space”, with the nearest free spaces; nothing was charged.
  • In the demo no space really waits for you, and no real money moves.

Questions

Can I cancel the hold? There is no cancel button: the hold runs out by itself after 15 minutes. If you take another space, this one is released at once.

What if I am late? After 15 minutes the space is released for others and hidden from you for 1 hour, so holds cannot be hopped from one to the next. If you know you need longer, choose more than 15 minutes: then you pay from now.

Do I have to be there to reserve? No. “Reserve · pay from now” works at any time during the hold.

Can I hold two spaces? No, one at a time.
