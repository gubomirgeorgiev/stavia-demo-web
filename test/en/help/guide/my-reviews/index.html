My ratings

What it is

The ratings you have given places, with where each stands, and the stays you can still rate. From here you change a rating, withdraw it or, if support took it down, contest the decision.

Where

Menu → My ratings. Also from a rated booking’s ticket (“Your rating”) and from a notification about a rating.

On the screen

  1. My ratings — at the top are the invitations “How was …?” for the stays you can rate: tap a star (1 to 5) and the rating opens with that many stars. Below them are your ratings, newest first.
  2. No ratings yet… — as at the start of the demo: after a stay you can rate the place here.
  3. You can change your rating at any time… — for example after a new visit, and withdraw it. If the place has answered, its answer stays, marked as one to an earlier version. If you delete your profile, your ratings stay as a former user’s, without your name.
  4. How ratings work — who rates and how we check, that every rating is published, the average, when we take one down, that ratings do not rank places, that there are no rewards, and what is shown about you.

A rating of yours: the place’s name (a tap opens its ratings), where it stands, and the rating itself. The “More” button (three dots) gives “Change” (while you may), “Contest the decision” (only for a rating taken down) and “Withdraw”.

Settings and options

  • Change: at any time, unless support took it down; the rating says “You can change it at any time.” After a change the rating stands for your latest confirmed stay at the place and says “changed on …”.
  • Withdraw: “Withdraw the rating?” — “It is deleted with its text and does not come back.”
  • Contest the decision: in the Test environment, for a rating support took down, once, within 6 months. You write why the decision is wrong and tap “Send”; another person of the team decides.

States

  • Published — shown on the place’s page.
  • Waiting for support (up to 72 hours) — a text with a link, a phone, an e-mail, a car’s plate or a personal ID number waits for a look before it is shown.
  • Taken down by support (only in the Test environment, where support decides) — “Why: …”, that a person decided, and that you can contest the decision within 6 months. After contesting: “Contested: another person of the team decides.”, then “Contest accepted: the rating is public again.” or “Contest looked at: the decision stays.”
  • No connection: “Not saved. Check the connection and try again.”

Questions

Why is there no invitation for my stay? An invitation comes once the visit is confirmed (a paid stay, the code read at the entrance, or your arrival reported) — once the stay is over (the car let out, or its time ended), until 14 days after the end, once per place: if you have rated it before, we do not ask again. The real places of the test world, and a place you work at, are not rated.

Will my low rating be hidden? No. Every rating is published, low or high; only a text with personal details or links waits for support.

Can I rate again? One rating per place: after a new visit, change the old one here. You can also withdraw it.
