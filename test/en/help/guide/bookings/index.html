My bookings

What it is

All your bookings and stays in one place: the upcoming ones and those running now, apart from the past ones. Each row opens the booking’s ticket.

Where

Menu → My bookings. While you have a confirmed booking or a stay that has not ended, the menu shows “1 active” beside the row.

On the screen

  1. Upcoming — bookings that have not ended yet and stays that are running, the earliest first. Requests waiting for the host are here too.
  2. Past — those that have ended, been cancelled or declined, the newest first.
  3. SAMPLE — the place’s tag (“TEST” for a real place in the test data). The row also has the place’s name, the time, the booking’s code and its status (“Confirmed”, “In progress”, “Completed”, “Cancelled”…). Tap the row to open the booking.
  4. Bookings are kept on this device only. — in the demo nothing is shared with other phones.

Above the choice, when you have a stay to rate, is the invitation “How was …?” with five stars (two invitations at most).

Settings and options

No settings here. “Upcoming” and “Past” only change what you see.

States

  • Nothing upcoming: “No upcoming bookings. Find a space on the map.” → “Find a space”.
  • Nothing past: “No past bookings yet.”
  • In the Test environment: “Bookings are on the demo’s server: every device that chose the same test driver sees them.”
  • Unpaid attempts (a payment left, a declined card) are not bookings and do not show here.

Questions

Why don’t I see the booking I started to pay for? Until it is paid it is not a booking: the space is released. Requests to a host who confirms do show, whatever came of them.

Do my bookings show on another phone? In the demo on the device, no. In the Test environment, yes, on every device with the same test driver.

Where is my “Park now” stay? Under “Upcoming” while it runs; its time reaches the latest you could stay.
