Where are you going?

What it is

Searching for your destination: an address, a place, a landmark or a Stavia car park, in one field. Once you choose a destination, the map shows the spaces around it, ordered by how far they are to walk.

Where

On the map: tap Where are you going? at the top. The back arrow at the top left returns to the map with nothing changed.

On the screen

  1. Address, place or car park — the field; the keyboard opens by itself. From the first letters, matching “Stavia spaces” and “Landmarks” show, and from 3 letters on, “Addresses and places” in Sofia too. The cross (“Clear”) empties the field.
  2. Choose on the map — “Move the map and put the pin where you’re going”. The map shows a pin in the middle and a “Where are you going?” sheet: move the map until the address shows under the pin, then “Show spaces here” (or “Cancel”).
  3. Popular places — ready destinations: NDK, Vitosha Boulevard, Alexander Nevsky Cathedral and others. Above them, once you have chosen destinations, is “Recent”.

The place or address you choose becomes your destination: on the map the heading becomes “Near …” and each row shows the minutes on foot. A car park chosen from “Stavia spaces” opens its space straight away. Under the addresses it says where they come from: “Addresses © OpenStreetMap contributors, search by Photon.”

Settings and options

No settings here. Only addresses in Sofia are searched.

States

  • Under 3 letters, no match: “Keep typing to search addresses too.”
  • Searching: a spinner under “Addresses and places”.
  • No address: “No addresses found in Sofia.”
  • No connection: “Addresses can’t be searched right now. Try again, or choose on the map.” Stavia spaces and landmarks are found offline too.
  • Places taken down by support, and listings not published yet, are not found.

Questions

Why can’t it find my address? Only addresses in Sofia are searched, from 3 letters. Try a shorter name, or “Choose on the map”.

Can I find a car park by name? Yes: Stavia’s places show under “Stavia spaces” with a “SAMPLE” or “TEST” tag. Tap one to open its space straight away.

What is in “Recent”? Your last destinations, up to 5. They are not kept after you close the app.
