A space

What it is

Everything about one space before you take it: what it is, what it costs, how you get in, the opening hours, the entrance, the ratings and whether your car fits. At the bottom there is always one main action.

Where

On the map: tap a row in the list or a price pin. It also opens from search (under “Stavia spaces”) and from the place’s link or QR code (see A place from its link). Close it with the cross at the top right, or with Back.

On the screen

  1. Guaranteed space — the space waits for you once you hold or book it. Instead it may say “Usually has space · no booking” (a car park without booking), “Held for you”, or why it is not free. Beside it is what the place has (“Covered”, “Lit”, “Video surveillance”, “Barrier”, staff, a charger); until Stavia has checked them, the note “As stated by the place; Stavia checks them on site.” stands under them. Under them is whether it is open now (“Open day and night”, “Open now · closes at …”).
  2. Price — per hour, and the most for a day (“… / h · max … / day”). Then “Getting in” (the host lets you in, a door code, reception, a QR code, a ticket on site), “How you pay”, “Entrance height” and the “Host rules”, if there are any.
  3. Hold free · 15 min — for “Now”: asks “How soon will you be there?” and keeps the space free for up to 15 minutes (see The free hold). Where the host does not offer the hold, the button is “Park · choose until when” and you pay from now. For “Later” the button is “Reserve · …” with the total, and leads to Payment.
  4. Park now · pay when you leave — park straight away with no end time, and pay for exactly the time when you leave (see Park now, pay when you leave).

Further down are “Opening hours” (if the place is not open day and night: when cars come in and go out, and whether a car may stay inside while it is closed), “Entrance” with its description and the distance from the address, “Getting in” explained, “Ratings from confirmed stays” (see Ratings from confirmed stays), “Report this place”, “Vehicle” (whether your car fits the entrance) and “EV charger”. For “Later” there is also “Cancellation”, with the time until which cancelling is free. At the very bottom, “Search around here” makes the place your destination, and “Directions” opens navigation to the entrance. A “TEST” place says: “Test data: a real place, but not this business’s listing. The price, spaces and hours are invented.”

Settings and options

No settings here. What you see depends on “Now” or “Later” on the map: for later the space shows the time you chose, the price line by line and cancellation.

States

  • A grey button: “Your vehicle is too tall”, “Taken for your time”, “Closed for part of your time”, “Hidden from you until …” (after a hold here ran out), and other reasons.
  • No car: “Add a car to book”.
  • A car park without booking: the main button is “Navigate to the entrance”.
  • Holding another space: “Switch spaces?” — “Switch spaces” goes on to the new one; the other is released once you take the new one, and hidden from you for 1 hour. “Keep the other one” keeps the other hold.
  • Someone took it just before you: “Someone just took this space” — nothing was charged, with the nearest free spaces.
  • Already yours: “Go to your hold” or “Go to your stay”.
  • A place taken down: “This place is not taking bookings right now.”

Questions

What does “Guaranteed space” mean? Once you hold it or pay, the space is yours for that time. In the demo the space is a sample, and at a “TEST” place no space is waiting for you.

What is the difference between the two buttons? “Hold free” keeps the space 15 minutes while you are on the way; then you choose until when and pay. “Park now · pay when you leave” starts the stay at once, with no end time.

How do I report a problem with the place? “Report this place” → a reason and a few words. In a test environment support decides, and you see the decision in the app. In the demo on this device a report reaches no one, and a “TEST” place takes no reports.
