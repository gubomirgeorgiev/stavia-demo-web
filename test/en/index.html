Your space is waiting.

Stavia shows free spaces in garages, hotels and office buildings. You see the price and where the entrance is before you book, pay in the app and get in by the instructions.

How it works for driversHave spaces to spare?

Three steps

1

Find

Search near where you are going and see the price, the hours and exactly where the entrance is.

2

Book

Pay in the app. The price is clear before you pay, and cancelling follows the place’s rules.

3

Park

Get in by the instructions, extend from your phone, and the documents arrive by themselves.

You pay only in the app

Stavia never asks for payment outside the app: not by phone, not with a link in the chat, not on paper at the place.

How to stay safe

For garages, hotels and office buildings

Empty spaces earn when you decide: you set the price, the hours and how many spaces you offer.
