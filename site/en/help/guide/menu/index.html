Menu

What it is

Everything beyond the map in one place: your profile, bookings, vouchers, documents, ratings, the legal pages and the app’s settings. A line’s current value stands on its right.

Where

On the map, the Menu button (three lines) at the top left. Close it with the cross at the top right (“Close menu”) or with the Map line.

On the screen

  1. Demo mode — “Sample data, no real payments.” The card with “Panel” opens the demo panel: the language, the test people, the demo time, the reset.
  2. My bookings — your bookings and stays. While you have a confirmed booking that has not ended, a coral badge stands on the right (“1 active”).
  3. Vouchers — on the right, the amount your vouchers still cover. Opens the vouchers from Stavia and from partners.
  4. Invoices — the sample invoices for your paid stays. In the Test environment this line is “My documents”.
  5. How it works — the three short pages from the first start, again.

Above these lines is your profile’s card: the name (“· test profile” for a person of the test world), the plate and the car. A tap opens “Profile”.

The lines in order: “Map”, “My code”, “My bookings”, “Vouchers”, “Invoices”, “My ratings”, “Invite a friend”, “My impact”, “How it works”, “Privacy”, “My data”, “Complaints and disputes”, “Terms for drivers”. After them: “Stavia Business” (“for hosts”, opens the demo’s business part), “Stavia Plus” (“Soon”, opens the plans) and the settings. At the bottom are the version and “Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors”.

Settings and options

  • Language — “Български” or “English”. A tap changes the language at once.
  • Appearance — “System”, “Light” or “Dark”; each tap moves to the next.
  • Vibration, Notifications, Sounds — switches. When you turn “Notifications” on, the phone asks once for permission.
  • Licences — the licences of the parts the app is made of.

States

  • In the demo on the device: there is no “Sign in”, and the documents are under “Invoices”.
  • In the Test environment: there is “Sign in” (or “Sign out” once you are signed in), “My documents” instead of “Invoices”, and “Guide”: this guide on the site.
  • Sign out: asks “Sign out on this phone” or “Sign out on every device”. Then: “Signed out. The phone is back in the demo.”
  • “Stavia Business” is only in the all-in-one demo. The separate Stavia app has no such line.

Questions

Why is there no number by “My bookings”? The number shows only while you have a confirmed booking or a stay that has not ended.

How do I change the test driver? “Demo mode” → “Panel” → “Test driver”.

I changed the language; what about my other settings? They stay. The language, the appearance and the switches are kept on this phone.
