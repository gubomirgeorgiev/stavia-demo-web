My data

What it is

Your rights over your data, in one place: you download everything Stavia keeps about you, ask for a correction and delete your profile. The full screen is for a person signed in as themselves in the Test environment.

Where

Menu → My data. “Your rights” in Privacy points to it too.

On the screen

The picture shows sample data, as in the Test environment. A person signed in as themselves sees three cards.

  1. Download your data — everything Stavia keeps about you.
  2. Download — gives two files to save or share: the data (JSON) and a page to read it, in Bulgarian and English.
  3. Correct your data — “Profile” opens your profile, where you correct your name, cars and invoice details.
  4. Ask for a correction — for anything else: you write what is wrong and how it should be, and tap “Send”. Support answers within a month.
  5. Delete your profile — first it says what is deleted at once and what stays, as long as the law asks.
  6. Delete the profile — asks you to type DELETE (or ИЗТРИЙ), then deletes the profile.

At the bottom: “Every download, correction and deletion is written down as a request about your data. More in Privacy.”

In the Test environment, not signed in: “My data is for a person signed in as themselves…” and a “Sign in” button.

In the demo on the device the screen has only an explanation: “In the demo on this device nothing of yours is on a server…”: the test people’s data stays on this phone, so there is nothing to download or delete.

Settings and options

No settings here. Every action is written down as a request about your data.

States

  • Correction sent: “Sent: support answers within a month.”
  • Profile deleted: “Your profile is deleted” — “This phone is back in the demo…”; “OK” takes you to the map.
  • Wrong word: “Type DELETE exactly to delete the profile.”
  • Too many: “Ten downloads a day. Try again tomorrow.”
  • No connection: “Not done. Check the connection and try again.”

Questions

What stays after deletion? The payment documents to 31 December of the 11th year, open cases until closed, a business’s records of your shifts for 3 years; your ratings stay for 24 months as a former user’s, without your name.

How fast does support answer a correction? Within a month. Your name, cars and invoice details you correct yourself in “Profile”.

Can I delete a test person of the demo? No. The test people are samples and their data is only on this device; “Reset all demo data” in the demo panel takes it back to the start.
