Demo panel

What it is

The desk for showing the demo: the language and appearance, the test people in each part, the demo time, what the demo simulates around the driver, and the reset. Everything here is sample data. Each app shows only the people and controls of its own parts; the all-in-one demo shows them all.

Where

Tap the yellow strip at the top of any screen (it is never hidden), or Menu → Demo mode → “Panel”. Close it with the cross at the top left.

On the screen

  1. Everything in the app is sample data… — bookings stay on this device only and are not shared with other phones or computers.
  2. Language — “Български” or “English”, at once.
  3. Appearance — “System”, “Light” or “Dark”.
  4. Test driver — the test drivers, each with their car and plate, labelled “TEST”. Tap a person to become them: the map, the bookings and the profile change hands.
  5. Open the driver app — goes to the map as the chosen driver.
  6. Stavia Business (hosts) — the people of the test businesses, each with their role and business: today’s arrivals, the listing, availability, the entrance. “Open Stavia Business” opens the business part as the chosen person.

Further down:

  • Support — Stavia’s team with their roles, and “Open the support console”. Below them is Component gallery: every shared part of the three apps in every state (colours, text, buttons, tags and rows, screen states, motion, sounds and vibrations) with “Text size” at 100 %, 115 % and 200 %.
  • Demo time — the demo’s day and time, with “+1 min”, “+5 min”, “+15 min” and “+1 hour”. The demo starts on a Friday at 18:10 Sofia time and runs on from there.
  • Next test payment — “Goes through”, “Declined” or “Unknown result”, for one payment.
  • The host — “Writes now” or “Writes in 10 s” about your latest paid booking.
  • Site staff or barrier — “Check in now”, “Check in in 10 s”, “Check out now”, “Check out in 10 s” for your latest stay, as staff at the entrance or a barrier would.
  • Another driver — “The last free space is taken just before you”.
  • Reset all demo data — takes the data and the demo time back to the start: “Demo data is back to its starting state.” The language and the appearance stay.

Settings and options

  • Who you are: one person in each part: a driver, a business’s person, a team member.
  • The clock only moves forward; the reset takes it back.
  • “In 10 s” is for trying notifications: minimise the app or lock the phone.
  • The separate apps show only their own parts; the “Open …” buttons are only in the all-in-one demo, and the gallery there and in the console.

States

  • In the Test environment: bookings are on the demo’s server and every device signed in to it sees them; each person shows the number or e-mail they sign in with. “The host” and “Another driver” are not there: a person answers in Stavia Business, and another phone takes the space. The reset takes the server’s data back for everyone.
  • Signed in as yourself: “Signed in as …: the device acts as that person.” The test people cannot be chosen until you sign out.
  • Nothing to simulate: “Pay for a booking first — the conversation is about a booking.” or “No stay to check: park first.”

Questions

Does the reset delete anything real? No. It only takes the demo’s sample data back to its start.

Why is the time at the top not the phone’s? The strip shows the demo time, which starts on a Friday at 18:10 and runs on from there.

How do I see a declined payment? Choose “Declined” under “Next test payment” and pay; the payment after it goes through again.
