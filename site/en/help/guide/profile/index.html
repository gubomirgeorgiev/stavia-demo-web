Profile

What it is

You on Stavia: your name, the cars you park, the test cards you pay with, and the details your invoices are made out to.

Where

Menu → the card with your name at the top. In the Test environment, once you are signed in, also My data → “Profile”.

On the screen

  1. SAMPLE — stands by the name when you are a person of the test world (“… · test profile”): the name and invoice details are samples and do not change.
  2. Standard — your plan (“Free”). Opens “Plans”.
  3. My vehicles — your cars: the plate, drawn as on the car (with the country’s sign), and the description. Tap a car to choose it: the circle on the right shows the one you use. Below them: “The spaces you see depend on the vehicle you choose, for example on its height.”
  4. My cards — your test cards (the brand and the last four digits, “TEST”). Tap a card to pay with it.
  5. Add a test card — opens “Test card”: the number, “MM/YY” and “CVC”. Only the public test numbers are taken; they are listed under “Test numbers to try”. “Add the card” adds it, and you pay with it.
  6. Invoice details — who your invoices are made out to: “Personal” or “Company” (with “Company”, “UIC (ЕИК)”, “Address” and “VAT no.” if it has one). The choice applies to the next payments.

At the bottom a note says whose profile it is: a sample person of the test world, or yours, kept on the server of the test environment.

Settings and options

  • Car: you choose one. In the Test environment there is also “Add a car”: “The plate’s country”, “Plate”, “Description (optional)”, “Height”, “Electric” and “Temporary car” (it goes by itself after a day you choose). You change or remove a car of your own from its menu (“Change the car”, “Remove”) or by swiping it to the left. Up to 5 cars per profile.
  • Card: test cards only. No real money moves.
  • Name (only when you are signed in as yourself in the Test environment): “Change the name” with the pencil, or “Add a name (optional)” if you gave none.
  • Invoice details (again only when signed in as yourself): “Change the invoice details”: “Personal” or “Company”, the company’s details and “E-mail for documents (optional)”.

States

  • A person of the test world: the name and invoice details do not change; their cars are samples.
  • A card refused: “That is not a card number.”, “The card has expired, or the month is wrong.”, “The security code is three digits.” A real card is refused and its number wiped at once: “That looks like a real card: only test cards are taken here. The number was neither sent nor kept.”
  • Company details refused: “Check the company: its name, a valid UIC (9 or 13 digits), the address and, if given, the VAT no. (BG and the UIC).”
  • E-mail refused: “Check the e-mail address.”
  • No connection: “Not saved. Check the connection and try again.”
  • No car: “No car yet. Add one to book or to pay in a zone.”

Questions

Why do some spaces disappear when I change the car? The spaces depend on the car you choose: a tall car does not see garages with a lower ceiling.

Can I add my real card? No. Only the public test numbers are taken, and a real card is neither sent nor kept.

Why can’t I add a car in the demo? On this device the test people’s cars are used. Cars are added in the Test environment, where they are kept on the server.

Does the e-mail for documents send anything? Not in the test environments. Receipts and invoices are always in the app.
