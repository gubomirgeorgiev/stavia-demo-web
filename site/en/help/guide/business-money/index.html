Money

What it is

Your business’s money from Stavia: the next payout and the account it goes to, the month with its whole sum (parking less refunds less the commission is what the place is owed), the month booking by booking as a spreadsheet, the commission invoices and every document in your name. All of it is a test: no real money moves and the documents are samples.

Where

Stavia Business → More → Money (on a laptop, in the menu on the left). The owner, the manager and the accountant see it. The accountant has it straight in the bottom bar, and their Stavia Business opens here.

On the screen

The picture shows sample data, as in the Test environment.

  1. Test money: … — no real payments; the invoices are samples, not tax documents. Beside it is the “TEST” tag.
  2. Next payout (test) — the amount the place will receive, “On …, when the month closes, to the account ···· …” (the account once it is set in the registration), and whether you are registered for VAT.
  3. Month — one of the last 12 months; the sum below and the spreadsheet are for the month you choose (the invoices and documents are for every month).
  4. Download the month booking by booking (CSV) — the arrow button beside the month: a spreadsheet with code, date, parking, refunded, commission, owed to the place and “Test data”.
  5. Owed to the place — in the month’s sum: the number of paid bookings, “Commission now: … %”, then “Drivers paid for parking”, “Refunded”, “Stavia’s commission (with VAT)”, “Owed to the place” and “Paid out”, and under them the sum written out: “… − … refunded − … commission = … owed to the place”.

Further down the screen:

  • Was the payout for this month what you expected? — under a closed month: “Yes”, “No” or “Not sure”. A “no” gets an answer from the team.
  • Commission invoices — number, month, amount without VAT, VAT and total.
  • Documents — the filters “All”, “Statements”, “From Stavia”, “To drivers” and “Agreements”; a tap opens the document to read, print or send.

Settings and options

No settings here. You choose the month and the kind of documents. The account for the payouts is set in the registration, step “2. Check and bank account”.

States

  • In the demo on the device: “Money is kept on the shared server: this version keeps everything on the phone.”
  • No invoice yet: “None yet: each month’s invoice comes on the first of the next.”
  • The spreadsheet button is greyed out when the month has no paid or refunded bookings.

Questions

When does the money come? After the month closes, on the first of the next, to the account from the registration. In the test environments no real money moves.

How is the amount owed worked out? What drivers paid for parking, less what was refunded to them, less Stavia’s commission (with VAT). The sum is written out under the month.

What do I give the accountant? The month’s spreadsheet and the documents under “Statements” and “From Stavia”. You can invite them with the “Accountant” role: they see Money, Reports, How you are doing and Help, but not the bookings or the entrance.
