Booking

What it is

The ticket of one booking or stay: how far it has got, the code for the entrance, how you get in, the entrance, what was paid, help, messages with the host and cancelling. Its title is “Booking” with its code (ST-1001, say).

Where

Menu → My bookings → the booking. It also opens by itself after paying (“Paid. The space is yours.”) or after “Start · pay when you leave”, from the chip above the map and from the booking’s notifications.

On the screen

  1. Parked since … — a “Park now, pay when you leave” stay that is running: the big amount so far, “so far · you pay when you end the stay”, and until when you can stay. For a paid booking this is “Confirmed” and “Starts at …” before the start, then “Stay in progress” with the time left.
  2. End and pay — ends the stay and charges the test card for exactly the time. If you pay at the place, instead of the button it says “You pay at the place when you leave: …”.
  3. Show at the entrance — the code for the way in and out; it appears 15 minutes before the start and changes every 15 seconds (“live · … · changes every 15 s”). Tap it, or “Full screen, brighter”: the code grows, the screen gets brighter, and “Show the code in digits” (or a swipe sideways) turns it over to digits with your plate. Once you are in, the code gets smaller: “Code for the way out” → “Show”.
  4. Doesn’t scan? Say or show this code: — the backup code in digits and your plate, for the staff at the entrance.

Further down are “In and out” (the paid time, and when the place confirmed you in and out, by QR code, plate or staff), “Getting in” (the code or instructions, shown 15 minutes before the start, always with a note that the demo does not control doors or barriers, or, where the host lets you in, that the door shows as open only when the host confirms), the place with “Time”, “Vehicle”, “Booking”, “Payment” and “Access”, and “Entrance” with “Navigate to the entrance”. Once paid there is “Paid”: the price line by line, “Test payment: no money moved.”, until when cancelling is free, the documents (in a test environment) and, after the stay, the invitation to rate it. At the bottom are “Messages with the host” (see Messages with the host), “Help with this booking” and, before the start, “Cancel booking”.

You’re in. Once the car is recorded as in, the card “You’re in” is at the top: the place and since when you are in, “To your space” (the place’s instructions), “Your time” (“Paid until … · … left”), “After your time” (the overstay rule), “Leaving” (show the code; with “Park now” also “End and pay”; when paying at the place, how) and the buttons “Messages with the host” and “Help with this booking”. If the app is open when staff let you in, the booking opens by itself on this card, with a tick.

While a paid stay runs, “Extend your stay” opens “More time”: until when, with the price, if the space is free; otherwise why not (“Can’t be extended past …” → “Why?”).

Settings and options

No settings here. “Help with this booking” asks “What happened?”: “I can’t get in”, “Someone is in my space”, “I’m running late” (before the start) and “Leaving, but no check-out came” (once the stay has started or you are in). The request goes to support with the booking.

States

  • A request to a host who confirms: “Waiting for the host” — “The host answers by … Nothing is charged until they confirm.”; “Withdraw request”. Then “Declined by the host” or “Lapsed” — “Nothing was charged.” and “Find another space”.
  • The payment is being checked: “Checking the payment” — don’t pay again. If the bank asks: “Your card’s bank asks you to confirm this payment.” → “Confirm with the bank”.
  • A problem reported: it stays marked until support solves it.
  • The car stayed past the end: “Extra time” — the amount and why, paid from your card.
  • Checked out: “Time has stopped. Nothing more is charged for this stay.”
  • Card declined after “End and pay”: “Card declined” and a button to pay again.
  • A “TEST” place: “TEST · no space is waiting for you at this place.”
  • The gate is closed when the stay ends: “Collect the car by …” (15 minutes before the end) and “The gate closes at …”.

Questions

Does the code open the barrier? No: “A sample code: in the demo it opens no barrier.” Staff in Stavia Business (“Entry”) confirm you in and out, or, in the demo, the demo panel → “Site staff or barrier”. You get a notification each time.

Can I cancel? Before the start: “Cancel booking”. Until the free-cancellation time you get the amount back (test); after it, not. A “Park now” stay is not cancelled but ended.

I’m leaving and it says “not confirmed yet”. The place has not confirmed you out yet. Ask the staff, or tap “Help with this booking” → “Leaving, but no check-out came”: support sees it at once.
