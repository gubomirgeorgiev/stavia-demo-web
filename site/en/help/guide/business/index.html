Today

What it is

Your places’ day on one screen: how many bookings and how much money today, how many spaces are taken, what needs attention, who is arriving, who is inside and who has left. The days ahead are under Bookings.

Where

Stavia Business opens here: Today is first in the bottom bar (on a laptop, in the menu on the left). The owner, the manager, reception and the staff at the entrance (the “Entrance” role) see it. The accountant has no Today: their Stavia Business opens on Money. With several places you choose the place at the top (on a laptop, in the “Business” card of the side menu) or “All places”; then each booking names its place.

On the screen

The picture is from a phone. Over the title is the place, and on the right the icons for the guide and for your account (your name, your role, Privacy, signing in and out).

  1. All places — with several places: you choose one or all. The choice holds for Today; Places and Availability show the chosen one (with “All”, the first).
  2. Free now — the dark card with the day: how many bookings start today and (for the owner and the manager) what the places earn from them; on the right, how many of the spaces offered to Stavia are free now.
  3. Let in by plate — opens Entry with the cursor in the field “Backup code or plate”: type the car’s plate (or the backup code) and “Check”.
  4. +1 without Stavia — notes a car let in outside Stavia, for the place’s own records. You choose the place (if you have several) and, if you like, a plate; “Note the car” adds it under “Inside without Stavia”.
  5. Needs attention — requests to answer (with the time to answer by; only for those who manage bookings, not the entrance), problems support has not resolved yet, and new messages from drivers. A tap opens the booking or the conversation.
  6. Arriving — who comes before the day ends, with the hours, or “Held free until …” for a driver on the way. “All bookings” opens Bookings.

A car still there after the booking and its grace time comes first, in red: “… · … min after the end”, “Overstaying · write to the driver”. For a private space, and at a place with many spaces, “My space is taken” there sends a report to support.

Further down:

  • Inside — who is inside: “Inside since …”, or “Started · not checked in” when the time has started and the car is not in yet.
  • Inside without Stavia — the cars noted with “+1 car without Stavia”. “It left” asks what was paid at the place: “Paid in cash”, “Paid on the place’s terminal” or “Nothing paid here”, and the amount. The owner, the manager and reception note a payment; the entrance only “Nothing paid here”. The money is the place’s and the receipt comes from its own till; nothing goes through Stavia.
  • Ended in the last 12 hours — those who left or finished.
  • Shift — who is on shift now. “Start my shift” starts yours with one tap: the place’s notifications come to you, and if the last shift left a note you read it first (“I have read it”). “End my shift” shows how many cars you let in and out and how many problems there were, and asks for a note for the next shift.
  • Reports — for those who see them: leads to the reports.

Each row shows the time, the driver’s name, the car’s plate and the booking’s code; a tap opens the booking.

On a laptop the screen is laid out as the design canvas draws it: on the left the dark menu (the business and the place at the top, then the parts with their counts — today’s bookings, the average rating — and at the foot Settings, Help and your name); at the top right “+1 car without Stavia” and “Let in by plate”; under the title four cards — Revenue today (and the change on the same day last week), Bookings today (past, inside, to come), Taken now (with a bar of how full) and the dark Next payout; then on the left Arriving and Inside, on the right Needs attention, Occupancy today (by the hour: grey past, black now, light booked ahead) and Shift.

Settings and options

The place (with several places). The screen refreshes itself every 15 seconds: requests run out, stays start and end with the clock.

States

  • Nothing waiting: “No requests, problems or messages waiting.”, “No one is inside.”, “No one else is coming today. The days ahead are under Bookings.”
  • The first booking: for a week after a new business’s first paid booking, and while it is still the only one, “Your first booking” sits over the lists, with its code, the place and the hours.
  • Two questions: in the Test environment, once every 90 days, the person who decides for the business sees a card with a 0–10 question and a field for one suggestion. “Not now” closes it; a person of the team reads every answer.
  • Next payout: in the Test environment the owner and the manager see on a laptop the card “Next payout · …” with the amount and “A test payout, no real money”.
  • At the foot it says whose the data is: in the demo, “Sample data on this device only…”; in the Test environment, on the demo’s server, shared by every device.

Questions

Why can’t I see the day’s money? Only roles with the right to the money see it: the owner and the manager. Reception and the entrance see the number of bookings.

What if I don’t answer a request in time? The request lapses: nothing is charged and the driver is offered other spaces. “Needs attention” shows the time to answer by.

What is “+1 car without Stavia” for? For cars you let in yourself, without a Stavia booking: for your own records and the shift’s count. Stavia takes nothing from them.
