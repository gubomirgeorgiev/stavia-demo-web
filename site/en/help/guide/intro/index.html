How it works

What it is

Three short pages about Stavia: you find a space by where you are going, hold it free while you are on the way, and get in with a code or by the instructions. Each page has a small moving picture of the idea.

Where

It appears by itself the first time the app starts, before the map. After that: Menu → How it works.

On the screen

  1. Skip — closes the intro at once. On the first start you go to the map, and the intro does not appear by itself again.
  2. Find a space by where you are going — the first of the three pages. Above the title, “1 of 3” shows how far you are.
  3. Search for an address or a place, or simply point on the map… — how you search. The picture below shows spaces with their price per hour and your destination in the middle.
  4. Next — the next page. On the last page the button is “Get started” and closes the intro.

The other two pages: Hold it free for 15 minutes — a ring counting the minutes, with “Free”; Get in without fuss — a “Gate code” card where the code appears digit by digit, labelled “SAMPLE”. You can also swipe left and right between the pages; the dots at the bottom left show which one you are on.

Settings and options

No settings here. If the phone is set to reduce motion, the pictures stay on their last frame.

States

  • First start: after “Skip” or “Get started” you go to the map. In the Test environment the app then asks once whether it may count which screens you use; you change the answer in “Privacy”.
  • From the menu: “Skip” and “Get started” take you back to the menu.

Questions

Is there always a free 15 minutes? No: only where the host offers a free hold. There the place’s panel has “Hold free · 15 min”.

Will the code in the picture open the gate? No. It is a sample. The real instructions for getting in are on the booking’s ticket, and in the demo nothing opens a barrier.

How do I see the intro again? Menu → How it works.
