Listing

What it is

What drivers see about a place and on what terms they book: how to find the entrance and get in, when it is open, safety and comfort, the price and the rules. Changes reach drivers at once; bookings already made keep their price and terms.

Where

Stavia Business → Places → Listing on the place (the bar keeps Places marked). Also from the registration, step “6. Price and hours”. The owner and the manager see it.

On the screen

  1. Place — with several places, you choose whose listing you see.
  2. The place — the “SAMPLE” or “TEST” tag, the name, the address and how drivers get in (for example “The host lets you in” or “Door code”).
  3. Location, entrance point and how drivers get in are set with Stavia… — these three are not changed here; they are checked on site in the pilot.
  4. How to find the entrance — words for drivers, “In Bulgarian” and “In English”.
  5. How to get in (shown 15 minutes before the start) — the instructions for getting in, in both languages; the driver sees them shortly before the start. Under them: “The entrance has a height limit” and “Tallest vehicle that fits, cm”.
  6. Save changes — saves everything on the screen at once: “Saved. Drivers see it now; bookings already made keep their price and terms.”

Further down are the listing’s cards, described under “Settings and options”, and at the foot Drivers see: the price (“… / h · max … / day”) and the rules as drivers will read them.

Settings and options

  • Coming in and going out: “Open day and night”, or “Opens at” and “Closes at” by the half hour, “Closed on Saturday and Sunday”, and “A car may stay inside while it is closed”: drivers are told before paying when they cannot collect it. A more complex weekly pattern is set with Stavia and only shown here.
  • Safety and comfort: “Staff on site” (“Day and night”, or “Staff from” and “Staff until”), “Video surveillance”, “Barrier”, “Lit”, “Covered”. Drivers see these with “as stated by the place” until Stavia checks them on site.
  • Price: “Per hour, €”, “Most per day, € (empty: no cap)”, “Free first minutes”, “Shortest charged stay” (greyed out when there are free minutes) and “Charged in steps of”.
  • Booking rules: “Drivers choose time in steps of”, “How far ahead” (from “Right now only” to 90 days), “Longest stay”, “Free cancellation” (or “No free cancellation”), “Drivers may add time to a stay”, “Free 15-minute hold for drivers on the way”.
  • Confirm each booking for later: drivers send a request and nothing is charged until you confirm; you answer within 30 minutes and at least an hour before the start, or the request lapses. Bookings for now stay instant.
  • Coming in with a code, no booking: drivers with a profile show “My code” at the entrance, confirm on their phone and pay when they leave; staff scan it in Entry.
  • How drivers pay: “Online through Stavia” (always on for now), “Cash at the place”, “Card at the place (your terminal)”. You record a payment at the place in Entry when the car leaves. Under them is the commission: your tariff for bookings from Stavia’s search; for coming in with a code or by your QR code, 0 %, and for a payment through Stavia only its fee (a test value).
  • When a stay ends: “A private space: the owner comes back”, “Opening hours: the gate is locked”, “Day and night, with a way out” or “Many spaces: the next driver may be waiting”. Under the choice is the sentence the driver reads.
  • Spaces offered to Stavia (only for a place with several spaces): “Fewer spaces” and “More spaces”: how many of the place’s spaces you offer.

States

  • Not saved: “Enter a price above zero, like 2.50.”, “Enter a height between 120 and 500 cm, like 200.”, “Not possible: more spaces than that are already booked at some moment ahead.”
  • Hours in the way of a booking: “These hours would shut out or shut in bookings already made: …. Stavia never takes a booked space away.”
  • Words that lead away from Stavia: “For everyone’s safety, links, e-mail addresses, phone, card or account numbers and payment apps are not accepted here…”
  • In the demo the listing is sample data and stays on this device. In the Test environment changes reach the test driver app at once.

Questions

I changed the price. What about bookings already made? They keep the price and terms they were made with. The new price applies to new bookings.

How do I change the address or how drivers get in? Not here: they are set with Stavia. Write to support from Help.

Will I lose bookings if I switch on “Confirm each booking for later”? Places with instant booking get more bookings, and a request with no answer lapses. Bookings for now stay instant.
