Sign in with e-mail

What it is

Signing in with an e-mail and a password, for owners, managers and accountants in Stavia Business and for Stavia’s team. Here you also make a new account with an e-mail, and a new password if you forgot the old one; both go through a 6-digit code. It works only in the Test environment.

Where

In the Test environment, while you are not signed in:

  • Stavia Business: the line with the name at the top → Sign in with e-mail; when registering a business, “Create a profile with an e-mail” or “I have a profile: sign in”;
  • the console: the line with the name at the top → Sign in as Stavia’s team.

On the screen

  1. E-mail — your account’s e-mail.
  2. Password — the eye button on the right (“Show the password”, “Hide the password”) shows or hides it.
  3. Sign in — signs you in. A member of Stavia’s team goes straight on to “Second factor”.
  4. Forgot the password — moves to “New password”: “We send a code. If the e-mail is someone’s, the code goes there.” — “Send a code”, then the code and the new password, and “Save and sign in”.
  5. New account with an e-mail — moves to “New account”: the e-mail and a password (at least 12 characters), “Create and send a code”, then the code and “Confirm”. Only in Stavia Business and only for people a business has invited by this e-mail: an e-mail no one invited makes no profile — “This e-mail has no profile here yet…” (the console has no such link: the team’s accounts are given by an administrator). A new business on Stavia (Stavia Business) opens the same form with the owner’s name first and leads to the registration of the company.

Once a code is sent: “We sent a code to …” and “In the test environments no e-mail is sent: the code is in the Test inbox, in the support console.” Under the button are the links to the other two: “Sign in with e-mail”, “Forgot the password”, “New account with an e-mail”.

On a laptop, in Stavia Business and in the console, the screen is laid out as on the design canvas: on the left a dark half with “stavia business” (or “stavia console”) and what the app is — “Your place, your money, your people. In one place.” or “The Stavia team’s console.”; on the right the form is on a card. In Stavia Business, under it: “At the entrance or at reception? Sign in with your phone, the way your manager invited you.” with “Sign in with a phone”.

Settings and options

  • Password: at least 12 characters for a new account and a new password.
  • A new account for a business (from the registration): first “Your name”: “Your name and e-mail. A code comes to the e-mail (in Test, to the Test inbox).” After confirming, you carry on with the registration.

States

  • Wrong e-mail or password: “The e-mail or the password is wrong.” The same answer for an e-mail that has no account.
  • Other refusals: “That does not look like an e-mail address.”, “The password must be at least 12 characters.”, “The e-mail is not confirmed yet: enter the code we sent.”, “The code is wrong or has expired.”, “Too many tries. Wait a minute and try again.”, “Write your name.”, “It did not work. Check the connection and try again.”
  • Signed in: “Signed in as …” and back to where you were.
  • In the demo on the device there is no signing in: nothing is sent.

Questions

Where is the code? In the test environments no e-mail is sent: the code is in the Test inbox in the support console.

Why is the answer the same for a wrong password and an unknown e-mail? So that no one can find out which e-mails have an account.

I work at the entrance; do I sign in here? No. Entrance staff sign in with a phone and a code: the line with the name at the top → “Sign in with a phone”.
