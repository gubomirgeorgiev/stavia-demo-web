Registration

What it is

How your business and its first place come onto Stavia: nine steps, each saved as you go, in any order where one does not need another. Drivers see the place only after Stavia’s review. Everything is a test: the company check is simulated, the terms are a draft and no real money moves.

Where

A business with no place yet opens straight here. Otherwise, in the Test environment: your name at the foot of the side menu (on a phone, the person icon at the top right) → “Register a business · TEST” or “Registration”; Places → “Continue the registration” or “Add a place”. The business’s owners and managers take the steps.

On the screen

Until you sign in with a profile, the screen shows only the start:

  1. Your business on Stavia — what lies ahead.
  2. Nine steps… — the company, its check and bank account, the data for DAC7, invoicing, the place with its entrance and photos, the price and hours, staff, a test booking, and Stavia’s review.
  3. TEST: … — the check is simulated, the terms are a draft awaiting the lawyer, and no money moves.
  4. Create a profile with an e-mail — a new profile for the business.
  5. I have a profile: sign in — signing in with an e-mail and a password.

Once signed in, the progress ring is at the top: “Steps done: … of 9” and “Each step is saved as it is done; the last one sends the place to Stavia.” Once the place is published, the ring becomes a tick with “Your place is published”. Under it is the Steps card: the nine steps, each with its number (a green tick when done) and how far it has got. A tap opens the step.

Settings and options

The nine steps:

  • 1. The company — the name, the UIC (ЕИК; 9 digits, 13 for a branch), a VAT number if “Registered for VAT”, the registered address, “Represented by”, a person to contact (Stavia calls this phone; drivers never see it) and “I accept the terms for businesses” with “Read the terms”.
  • 2. Check and bank account — the IBAN of the account for the payouts; only its last characters are kept. In Test only the three test accounts in the list are taken (the check passes, more documents are asked for, or it fails); a number that could be a real account is refused and not kept.
  • 3. Data for DAC7 — nothing new to type: what Stavia reports to НАП once a year, from the company and the places’ addresses. Nothing is reported from Test.
  • 4. Invoicing — who invoices the drivers: “Stavia, in our name” (with the first invoice number) or “We issue them ourselves”.
  • 5. The place — the name, the address, the entrance as a point on the map apart from the address, how to find the entrance, how drivers get in, the spaces, the price an hour and photos (no people and no number plates; Stavia checks each). A new place is a draft.
  • 6. Price and hours — opens Listing.
  • 7. Staff — optional: an invitation by phone or e-mail with a role. In Test the invitation is in the Test inbox.
  • 8. Test booking — Stavia’s test driver books the place for the next hour, free; you let the car in and out in Entry.
  • 9. Review by Stavia — “Send to Stavia” when everything is ready. A person checks the company, the place, its entrance and photos; for the first places, with a visit. “Still missing” lists what is not ready.

The steps with fields (the company, invoicing, the place) are saved with “Save”; a new place with “Create the place (draft)”. The steps after the first open once the company is saved; the price, the test booking and the review once there is a place. The test booking is only for a draft place.

States

  • Refused: for example “This is not a UIC (ЕИК): 9 digits (13 for a branch) with the right check digit.”, “Something is missing: fill in every field.”, “Accept the terms for businesses.”, “Only the business’s owners and managers can do this.”, “It was not saved. Try again.”
  • Review: “Stavia is checking it”; “Returned for changes: …” with support’s note; “Published: drivers find and book it.”
  • In the demo on the device no profile can be made: the registration works only with the server, in the Test environment.

Questions

Do I have to take the steps in order? No. Each is saved at once. Only the company comes first: without it the other steps do not open. The review needs a checked company, the terms accepted, invoicing chosen, a photo of the place and the test booking let in; “Still missing” says what is not done.

Why a test booking? To see how a booking arrives and how the car is let in from Entry before a real driver comes. Nothing is paid; the car is no one’s.

Can I change the address after the review? Not yourself, once the place is sent to Stavia (unless it is returned for changes): “Stavia has seen this place: its location and how drivers get in are now fixed; ask support to change them.”
