Sign in

What it is

Signing in with your mobile number and a code, no password. A new number becomes a new driver’s profile in two steps. Signing in works only in the Test environment, where there is a server; in the demo on the device you are always one of the test drivers.

Where

In the Test environment: Menu → Sign in, or My data → “Sign in”. In Stavia Business the staff at the entrance sign in here: the line with the name at the top → “Sign in with a phone”.

On the screen

  1. With your mobile number and a code: no password.
  2. Mobile phone — your number. The hint in the field is an invented number.
  3. Send a code — sends a 6-digit code. In the test environments no text message is sent: the code is in the Test inbox, in the support console.

After the code: “We sent a code to …”, the “6-digit code” field and “Sign in”. A new code (“Send a new code”) comes after a minute; until then the button counts down, “New code in … s”. “Another number” takes you back to the number. Once in: “Signed in as …”.

A new number: “… is new to Stavia.” You write “Your name (optional)”, tick “I am 18 or older” and tap “Create profile”; by creating it you accept the terms for drivers (a draft), and the “Terms for drivers” and “Privacy” links are there. “Not now” stops.

The profile is ready: “Your profile is ready” and two more steps that can wait, “Your car” and “How you pay” (with “Add”). The car’s form opens by itself, then the test card’s; you can close either. Below them, “New: come in with your code” with “Show me my code”, and “To the map”.

Settings and options

  • The name is optional; if you give one, at least two letters. In the test environments use an invented name.
  • 18 or older: without the tick the profile is not created.

States

  • In the demo on the device: the menu has no “Sign in” and no code is sent.
  • Refusals: “That does not look like a mobile number.”, “The code is wrong or has expired.”, “Too many tries. Wait a minute and try again.”, “Stavia is for people who are 18 or older.”, “It did not work. Check the connection and try again.”
  • Signed in: the device is that person, and the demo panel’s test people cannot be chosen. Menu → Sign out asks “Sign out on this phone” or “Sign out on every device”; then the phone is back in the demo.

Questions

Where is the code if no text message comes? In the test environments, in the Test inbox in the support console.

Which number do I sign in with? The test people sign in with the numbers shown in the demo panel. A new number becomes a new profile.

Why does a new number not become a profile in the Stavia Business app? There the staff at the entrance sign in with a phone; for a new number it says “This number is no one’s yet. Here the test people sign in: their numbers are in the demo panel.”
