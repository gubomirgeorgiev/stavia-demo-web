Add a car

What it is

You add a car with its plate, so the staff at the entrance find it. The country and the plate are two fields; the plate is recognised as you type it and shown as the plate on the car. It works for every Bulgarian plate — ordinary, electric cars’, foreign residents’ (ХХ, ХН), diplomatic, military, transit, dealers’ temporary, personalised — and for plates of other countries.

Where

In the Test environment: Profile → My vehicles → “Add a car”. It also opens by itself when you book, park or pay in a zone without a car. You change a car of your own from its menu → “Change the car”.

On the screen

  1. The plate’s country — Bulgaria is chosen. Tap to choose another: the list starts with Bulgaria and its neighbours, then the rest by name; you search in Bulgarian or English (“Search for a country”). If the country is not there, “Another country”.
  2. Plate — written as on the plate, in Cyrillic or Latin, with or without spaces. Under the field the plate appears (the blue band with the country’s sign, green characters for an electric car, red for diplomatic, blue for ХХ, a red strip for transit and temporary plates) with its kind in words: “Bulgarian plate”, “Transit plate”, “A plate from Germany”…
  3. Description (optional) — e.g. “grey hatchback”: you see it under the plate in “My vehicles”.
  4. Height — “Car, up to 1.5 m”, “SUV or tall, up to 1.95 m” or “Van, up to 2.3 m”: places with a lower ceiling are not shown.
  5. Electric — turns on by itself for an ЕА or ЕМ plate; turn it on yourself for an older electric car with an ordinary plate.
  6. Temporary car — for a rental or a car on transit plates: under it “Kept until … inclusive” appears (a week ahead; tap to choose another day, up to a year ahead). After that day the car leaves your profile by itself, with its plate. For a transit plate it turns on by itself; you can turn it off.
  7. Save the car — saves the car and chooses it.

Under the plate it says: “Check it is exactly as on the plate: it is how the staff at the entrance find your car.”

Settings and options

  • Country: from the list, or “Another country”.
  • Plate: a Bulgarian plate is kept in Cyrillic with spaces between its groups (“СВ 1234 КА”); a foreign one in Latin letters as typed (“M-AB 1234”), without accents (Ü becomes U).
  • Temporary car: the last day is changed from “Change the car”; turn “Temporary car” off and the car stays until you remove it. In “My vehicles” the plate has “temporary, until …” under it.
  • An unusual plate: if a Bulgarian plate looks like no known kind, “Save the car” first asks: “It is exactly as on the plate · Save”. Tap it if the plate really is like that.

States

  • A letter Bulgarian plates do not have: “Bulgarian plates have only the letters А, В, Е, К, М, Н, О, Р, С, Т, У and Х.” (at once, as you type).
  • A foreign plate with a letter that is not Latin: “Only Latin letters (A–Z) and digits (0–9).”
  • Too short or too long: “A plate has 2 to 12 letters and digits.”
  • Not like a Bulgarian plate: “It does not look like a Bulgarian plate (for example СВ 1234 КА). If it is exactly as on the plate, save it as it is.” Likewise for a plate with no area code, and for the small digits of the year on ХХ and diplomatic plates.
  • A day out of range: “Choose a day from today to a year ahead.”
  • You have this car already: “You already have a car with this plate.”
  • Five cars: “Up to 5 cars per profile. Remove one first.”
  • No connection: “The car was not saved. Check the connection and try again.”
  • In the demo on the device: instead of the fields, “Cars are added in a test environment, where they are kept on the server…”

Questions

Why in Cyrillic? Bulgarian plates have only 12 letters, which look the same in Cyrillic and Latin, so “CB 1234 KA” and “СВ 1234 КА” are one plate.

A rental car, or one with a transit plate? Turn on “Temporary car” and choose its last day: after it the car and its plate go by themselves. For a transit plate this is on already.

When exactly does it go? The morning after its last day, when the server clears old data. Until then the car shows “temporary, until …”.
