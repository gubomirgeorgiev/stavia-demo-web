Map

What it is

The driver app’s first screen: the map of Sofia with Stavia’s spaces, the city’s street zones and charging stations, and under it the list of spaces for now or for later. From here you search, compare, hold and book without leaving the map.

Where

The driver app opens on the map; on the first start, the pages of How it works come before it. From other screens you come back with Back, or from the menu → Map. The Menu button (three lines) at the top left opens the menu.

On the screen

  1. Where are you going? — the search: an address, a place, a car park or a point on the map (see Where are you going?). Once you have a destination, its name and your time show here, and the cross (“Clear destination”) removes it.
  2. Map layers — turns “Paid street zones” (the blue and green zones) and “Charging stations” on and off. A coral dot on the button means a layer is on.
  3. My code — the code you show at an entrance with staff, with a booking or without one (see My code).
  4. My location — shows where you are. The first time, the phone asks for permission. With it, the list becomes “Spaces around you”, closest first. Once you have a destination, “Back to your destination” appears beside the button.
  5. Now — spaces for right away: hold one free for 15 minutes, or park straight in. Beside it, Later opens “When are you parking?”: a day, “Arrive”, “Leave” and “Show spaces”. The time you chose takes the place of “Later”; tap it again to change it.
  6. Filters — which spaces you see (see Settings and options). The number that are on shows on the button.

Under the heading (“Places nearby”, “Spaces around you”, or “Near …” once you have a destination) is the list. Each row shows the name with a “SAMPLE” or “TEST” tag, how far it is, how you get in and the price: “per hour” for now, “Total” for later. “15 min free” means the space can be held free. Tap a row, or a price pin on the map, to open the space (see A space). Further down are “Other options: no booking” (car parks that usually have space) and “Not free right now” (“Not free for your time” for later) with the reason. Before a destination there are quick destinations under the heading (NDK, Vitosha Boulevard…); after one, “Closest” and “Cheapest”.

If your destination is in the blue or green zone, a card above the list says “Your destination is in the …” with the tariff and the longest stay. It, like a zone on the map, opens the zone’s rules and “Pay for parking here” (a simulation; see Zone · simulation). Hold a finger on the map to set a destination at that point. A charging station on the map shows its free chargers and their price; they are not booked through Stavia.

Settings and options

  • Later: the day you arrive is up to 7 days ahead, the day you leave up to two weeks after it. We first suggest the second round quarter hour from now, for three hours (18:10 becomes 18:30–21:30).
  • Filters: “Only spaces kept for me”, “Fits my car”, “With charger”, “Instant booking only”, “Staff on site”, “Video surveillance”, “Covered”, and “List only”, which hides the map: easier with a screen reader or keyboard. “Done” closes them.
  • Layers: the zones are on from the start; charging stations show on their own if your car is electric.

States

  • Nothing for now: “No space nearby can be held right now. Try booking for later, or move the map.” For later: “No guaranteed space is free for this time. …”
  • No location: the line under the heading says how to see spaces around you. Refused: “Without location access the map can’t show where you are.”; if access is off, a “Settings” button.
  • A hidden space: after a hold ran out, “1 space is hidden from you until … after a hold ended”.
  • Few spaces nearby: “No place nearby? Tell us where you park” → “Tell us”. In the demo on this device it reaches no one; in a test environment it goes to Stavia.
  • Something running: a dark chip above the list (“Held until …”, “Parked · …”, “Parked until …”, “Booked for …”, or the time left in a zone) leads straight to it.
  • Every space is sample data on this device; “TEST” places are real places in Sofia that are not those businesses’ listings.

Questions

Why don’t I see spaces around me? Allow your location with “My location”. The sample spaces are in Sofia: outside it the list is around the map’s centre. You can also search for a destination or hold a finger on the map.

Why is a space grey? It is taken, closed for your time, or its entrance is too low for your car. The row says which.

Is a zone paid through Stavia? No. The map shows the zone’s rules, and paying in Stavia is only a simulation. For real you pay the city: a text, or the Sofia Plus or Urbo app.
