Payment

What it is

Paying for a booking for later. While you pay, the space is held just for you for 10 minutes. The payment is a test: no real money moves.

Where

On the map, choose “Later” and your time, open a space and tap Reserve · … (see A space). Going Back before paying gives the space back to other drivers.

On the screen

  1. We’re holding this space for you for … more. — what is left of the 10 minutes. Under it is the place: the name with its tag, the time, your plate and the price line by line (“Parking, …”, “Stavia fee (sample)”, “Vouchers”, “Total”).
  2. Use my vouchers — on by default: “… off from your vouchers. What is left stays in your profile.” Off: “Your vouchers stay in your profile for another time.”
  3. Test card … — the card you pay with; “Change” picks another or adds a test card. Under it: until when cancelling is free, and what you pay if the car stays past the end.
  4. I ask for the parking to start at the chosen time, within the 14 days to withdraw. I know that I lose the right to withdraw once the parking has been fully provided, and that if I withdraw earlier I pay for the time used. — the tick; without it the pay buttons are grey. It is never ticked in advance, and the confirmation e-mail repeats it. Under it are the buttons: “Pay with Google Pay · TEST” (Apple Pay on an iPhone), “Pay from the bank account · TEST” and the main “Pay … (test)”.
  5. By paying you accept the terms for drivers (a draft). — opens the terms for drivers.

After a successful payment the booking opens with “Paid. The space is yours.”

Settings and options

  • Vouchers: on or off for this payment. If they cover the whole amount, the button is “Confirm · paid with vouchers”.
  • Card: “Change” — which test card.
  • How you pay: the test card, the phone’s wallet or the bank account — all simulated.

States

  • The 10 minutes are up: “The hold ran out and the space was released. Go back and search again.”
  • The host confirms: “The host confirms this booking” — nothing is charged now, the host answers by a set time, and the test card is charged only once they confirm. The button is “Send request · pay once the host confirms”.
  • The same car is booked at this time: “… already has a booking at this time” → “Open the other booking”. You may carry on.
  • Card declined: “Card declined” — “Nothing was charged. The space stays held for you until … — try again.”
  • Paying: “Processing test payment…”
  • No connection (test environment): “No connection to the server. Nothing was paid; try again when you are online.”

Questions

How long do I have to pay? 10 minutes from “Reserve”. If you leave with Back, the space is released at once.

Is any money taken? No. The card is a test card, and the wallet and the bank account are simulated.

What if I cancel later? Free until the time it says here (2 hours before the start at most sample places): you get the amount back (test) and the vouchers go back to your profile. After that there is no refund.
