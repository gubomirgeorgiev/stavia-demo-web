Zone · simulation

What it is

Paid time in the city’s blue or green zone, as a text message would buy it: an hour at a time, for your car’s plate, up to the zone’s longest stay. Everything here is a simulation: it is not a valid payment to the city. The title is “Blue zone · simulation” or “Green zone · simulation”.

Where

On the map, tap a zone, or the “Your destination is in the …” card above the list. In the zone’s sheet, under “Pay for parking here”, tap Pay 1 hour · …. While the time runs, a chip above the list on the map (“Blue zone · 42:10”) and “Open” in the zone’s sheet lead here.

On the screen

  1. Not a valid payment to the city. For real: a text to …, or Sofia Plus or Urbo. — with the “SIMULATION” tag.
  2. left · until … — above it, the zone with “· paid” and the time left in large figures. If the zone stops charging before the end: “From … the zone does not charge: parking is free after that.”
  3. Parking paid for … in the … until …. (simulation) — the confirmation as a text message would bring it: from the zone’s number, one for each hour paid, with the time of the payment.
  4. One more hour · … — buys one more hour, until you reach the zone’s longest stay (2 hours in the blue zone, 4 in the green).
  5. Paid until — in the card at the bottom: “Vehicle”, “Zone”, “Started”, “Paid until” and “Paid” (the amount and how many hours).

If the car is paid for in the other zone too, under the time left is “Also paid in the …” with its time; tap it to go to that one.

Settings and options

No settings here. It is paid with the test card from your profile; no real money moves.

States

  • The longest stay: “That is the longest stay here (…). Move the car by …: the city takes no new payment for it in this zone until ….” — with the note on the mayor’s order (10 minutes with no new payment in the same zone).
  • Paid time ended: “Paid time ended at …” — “While the zone charges, an unpaid car can be clamped. Pay another hour or move the car.”
  • Five minutes before the end: a notification “…: paid until …” with “One more hour”, if another hour is possible.
  • In the zone’s sheet: “Parking here is free now.” outside the zone’s hours, “Free for your electric car” for an electric car, and “Sending…” while the payment goes through.

Questions

Is the payment valid? No. It is a simulation in the demo. For real you pay the city: a text with the car’s plate (in Latin letters) to the zone’s number, or the Sofia Plus or Urbo app.

Is a space guaranteed in the zone? No: “Street zone · no space guaranteed”. For a guaranteed space, tap “Guaranteed spaces nearby” in the zone’s sheet.

Do electric cars pay? A fully electric car with an electric-car plate, or registered with the city’s mobility centre (ЦГМ), parks free in the blue and green zones. The zone’s sheet says so, and still lets you pay if the car is not registered.
