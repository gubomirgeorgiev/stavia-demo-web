Vouchers

What it is

Your parking credit: vouchers from Stavia and from partners (shops and cafés that pay for your parking). They come off your next payment by themselves.

Where

Menu → Vouchers. On the right of the menu’s line is the amount your vouchers cover now.

On the screen

  1. Partners — switches between the vouchers from Stavia (“Stavia”) and those from partners.
  2. Valid until … — each voucher: who it is from, how much is left and until when it is valid. All are labelled “SAMPLE”.
  3. Vouchers come off your next payment… — those expiring soonest go first. At payment you can keep them for another time.
  4. Expired and used — vouchers no longer valid, in grey: “Expired on …” or “Used”.
  5. Add a code — opens “Voucher code”. Type the code and tap “Add”.

Settings and options

  • Stavia / Partners: which vouchers you see.
  • Code: the sample codes are written under the field (“Sample codes in the demo: …”). In the Test environment the code is checked on the server.

States

  • Added: “Voucher added.” The screen moves to its kind (Stavia or partners).
  • Refused: “No such code.”, “This code has already been added.” or “This promotion has ended or given all it could.”
  • Empty: “You have no Stavia vouchers yet.” or “You have no partner vouchers yet. Shops and cafés can pay for your parking.”
  • The amounts are samples and no real money moves.

Questions

Why did the voucher not come off? You may have chosen at payment to keep it for another time, or it has expired. Expired ones are under “Expired and used”.

Can I use two vouchers at once? Yes. They come off one after the other, the one expiring soonest first.

Where do I see how much the vouchers paid? On the invoice: “Paid with” says whether it was paid by card, by vouchers or both.
