Cookies and what the site keeps

This site

It sets no cookies and keeps nothing on your device. The language is in the address: /en/ is English.

Other companies

The site loads nothing from other companies: the font is ours, and there are no ads, counters or embedded maps. “Directions to the entrance” opens Google Maps only when you tap it.

Our server

The place’s page, the status and the company details ask Stavia’s server (Supabase, Frankfurt). The site’s host (GitHub Pages) sees your IP address, as with any site.

The apps

What the apps keep on your phone is in “Privacy”.
