Privacy

Draft: a lawyer checks this text before the pilot. Everything here today is test data.

Who answers for your data

Stavia. A place you park at answers for what it does with your first name and plate.

What we keep

Your profile, your bookings with the plate and the times, the chat with the place, and payments (only test ones in the demo).

Who sees it

The place sees only your first name and the plate. Support opens more only with a reason, and every opening is written down. We sell no data and show no ads.

How long

The details of a stay 13 months after it ends, the chat 6 months, payment documents as long as the law asks (until the end of the 11th year). Your cars as long as you keep them; a temporary car until its last day.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy, a correction or deletion, object, and complain to the Commission for Personal Data Protection (КЗЛД). Use “My data” in the menu, or write to support.

Why we may

To give you the service you asked for (a contract); to keep payment documents (the law); for safety and to stop fraud (a legitimate interest, assessed in writing); screens you use only with your yes (consent).

Who helps us

Supabase keeps the data in Frankfurt; Google Firebase delivers notifications, which carry no names or messages; OpenStreetMap draws the map and Photon finds places; both see the phone's address. Before the pilot each one is replaced or signs an agreement with us.

If you work at a place

Stavia sees your name, your role and whom you let in, so the log stays true. The camera reads the driver's code or plate on the phone; a plate with no booking is never kept.

How a business stands with us

To know whom to call, Stavia works out how each business is doing from its bookings, places and use of Stavia Business, its answers to our short questions and our talks with it. Only people of the team see it and act on it; it never changes ranking, prices, tariffs, payouts or whether a listing is shown. You can ask for your score and its reasons, or object, and then you are left out of it.

Which screens you use

Only with your yes: which screens open, without your name, plate or messages.

On this phone

The language and your settings, the test people chosen in the demo, your answer about screens and, if you allowed notifications, the address they reach this phone at. Nothing for advertising.
