Empty spaces earn.

When and as much as you decide: you set the price, the hours and how many spaces you offer, and drivers book and pay in advance.

How it works

1

Register your place

Your company, the place with its entrance on the map and photos, the price and how many spaces you offer. Stavia’s team reviews the listing before it is published.

2

Drivers book and pay

In the app, before they come. You see the bookings in Stavia Business on a phone and a laptop.

3

You get paid

With a monthly statement and an invoice for the commission.

What you could earn

An example, not a promise: 30 days a month, before Stavia’s commission. The commission is in the terms for businesses.

Questions

Who pays the commission?

The place, from the parking money, at the tariff in the terms for businesses. The driver pays the listing’s price and Stavia’s booking fee.

Can I offer spaces only at certain hours?

Yes. You set the hours and how many spaces you offer by day and hour, and you pause the listing whenever you like.

How do drivers get in?

As you decide: your person lets them in, with a code, at the reception or with a QR code.

What do I see about the driver?

Their first name and the car’s plate — enough to let them in.

What if a driver stays longer?

You choose the overstay rule in Stavia Business, and the driver sees it before booking.
